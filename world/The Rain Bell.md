# The Rain Bell

- **Type:** Item (sacred relic of [[The Lady of the Mere]])
- **Status:** Missing — publicly believed stolen. *Truth (GM only): hidden by [[Sister Maren]] under the hearthstone of her cottage at the [[Mere Shrine]], wrapped in an old wool habit.*
- **Owner:** The Mere Shrine
- **Weight:** about 30 pounds — one person can carry it, awkwardly

## Description
A bronze hand-bell the size of a bucket, green with age, cast with water-lilies and a crowned woman's face. Its clapper is a smooth river stone on a bronze chain. When rung, its note carries unnaturally far over water, and the lake answers with a single ripple from shore to shore.

## Properties
- It must be rung **at the Bellstone** to renew the pact. Traditionally that happens on Midsummer's Eve, but it works on any day: rain falls the following day.
- **In a fiftieth year — this year, 1147 — whoever rings it goes down into the lake as the Lady's new Voice.** (See [[The Lady of the Mere]].)
- Muffled in wool, it can be carried in silence. Unmuffled, it chimes at every jolt.
- It is unnaturally hard to damage; hammers ring off it.

## GM guidance
If someone is about to ring it without knowing the cost, give one warning sign first: the lake goes utterly still, the air turns cold, and a voice from the water asks, *"Will you come down to me?"* If they ring it anyway, it happens.

## History
- ~400 years ago — cast for the founders of Wendle.
- ~160 years ago — carried out of the chapel of [[Old Wendle]] as the water rose.
- 1097 — rung by [[Tam Ashby]], who then walked into the lake.
- The night before Midsummer's Eve, 1147 — taken from the bell-house by Sister Maren and hidden.
