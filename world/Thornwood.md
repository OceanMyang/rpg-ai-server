# Thornwood

- **Type:** Location (forest)
- **Status:** Parched and brittle — tinder in the drought
- **Location:** The western slopes of [[Merrowdale]], 2 hours' walk around the lake from [[Dunmere]]

## Description
Old oak, holly, and blackthorn, tangled and dim even at midday. The streams have dried to stony gullies and the moss has gone brown. Thirst makes the animals bold: deer come down to the lake at dusk, and wolves follow them.

## Features
- **Granny Nettle's hut** — a crooked hut of wattle and turf in a hollow, hung with drying herbs and little bone-and-glass charms. Beside it a spring still trickles into a stone trough — **the only living spring left in the valley**.
- **The path** is hard to find without a guide: a Wits roll, or follow the charms tied to branches (if you know to look for them). [[Wren]] knows the way.

## Dangers
- A pack of three starving wolves (rating 1 each).
- Fire: a campfire, a torch, or dry lightning could set the whole wood ablaze.

## Secrets (GM only)
- Nettle's spring still flows because [[Tam Ashby]] begged [[The Lady of the Mere]] to spare it. Nettle doesn't know why it flows.

## History
- 50 years ago — [[Granny Nettle]] leaves the village and settles here.
