# Spirit Stones (灵石)

- **Type:** Item (currency and fuel)
- **Where:** sect stipends, beast cores, old hoards, the pawnshop in the prefecture seat

Cloudy quartz, warm to the touch, about the size of a thumb joint. Low-grade is all anyone in [[Cangping Prefecture]] has ever seen.

## Uses
- **Fuel:** crushed in the hand, a stone gives **+2 Qi** instantly and crumbles to grit. This is the only battery in the setting and it is why a cultivator with three stones is dangerous out of proportion to their realm.
- **Breakthrough:** meditating on a stone gives +1 to a breakthrough attempt (the stone is spent either way).
- **Insight:** a cache of five or more, meditated on over a night, is +1 Insight. Once per cache.
- **Money, among cultivators:** 1 low stone ≈ 10 taels of silver. A grain merchant will not take one; a sect will.
- **Talismans and pills:** every talisman is written with stone-dust ink, which is why a [[Thunder-Paper Talisman]] costs what a family earns in a year.

## What is around
- The sect pays inner disciples **2 stones a month**; outer disciples get none and can buy one for 10 merit.
- The kiln-god niche in [[The Sunken Kiln]] holds **three**, hidden nine hundred years ago.
- A [[Corpse Moths]] nest yields a knot of grey beast-core grit worth about one stone if a sect will buy it.
- [[Ruan Shiqi]] has never owned one and has sold four to the seat's pawnshop, which is how he eats in winter.

## The temptation
Spirit stones are the one resource the player character can *make* with modern knowledge: they are assayed by warmth, graded by eye, and the seat's pawnbroker is bad at both. Sorting, arbitrage, and honest bookkeeping will make a Realm 1 richer than a Realm 3, and the host should let that work — money buys pills, manuals, cave nights and merit, and none of it buys a realm outright.
