# Tao Xiaomai (陶小麦)

- **Type:** Character (human boy, 11)
- **Status:** Alive
- **Realm:** 0 — Mortal (凡人), untested, unawakened
- **Location:** Tao's dye yard, [[Luyang Town]]
- **Stats:** Might −1, Grace +1, Wits +1, Heart 0
- **Abilities:** Climbing 1, Being where he should not be 2
- **Health:** 4/4

## Description
Small for eleven, quick, filthy, blue to the wrists like his mother. Barefoot in the ninth month and genuinely not cold. Front tooth chipped.

## Persona
- Relentlessly curious, no sense of danger, asks forty questions in a row.
- Attaches instantly to anyone who tells him something true.
- Shows off. Will absolutely tell the whole street about the stranger's glowing glass slab.
- Frightened of nothing except his mother's face when she is frightened.

## Wants
- To be taken up the mountain and made an immortal. He has decided this and mentions it daily.
- To be told how the glowing slab works.

## Knowledge
- Every roof, wall and gap in Luyang. Where Ruan posts the bounties. Which barge crews will let a boy ride.
- That the kiln-diggers stopped coming back for their pay.
- That a cart comes after the tax, and that the Sung boy went away on it, and that his mother goes quiet about it.

## Secrets (GM only)
- He has a **pure fire root (火天灵根)**. The root-stone will go red in his hands on Day 8, and every Hollow Night person present will stop talking.
- He has never been cold in his life and thinks that is normal.
- If the player character teaches him anything real — a technique, or just how to boil water and count — he is capable of Realm 3 in fifteen years. He is also eleven, and burning him takes nine days.

## Relationships
- [[Tao Liuniang]] — his mother.
- [[Ruan Shiqi]] — his hero; teaches him snares and tells him to go home.
- The player character — a marvel. He will follow them into the kiln if not physically prevented.

## History
- Tianyou 12 — born.
- Tianyou 20 — his father drowns. He does not talk about it.
