# Spirit Roots (灵根)

- **Type:** Lore — talent, and how the world sorts children
- **Mechanics:** a matched root turns a technique's +1 into +2; a mismatched element is +0

A spirit root is the shape a body lets qi take. It is fixed at birth, it cannot be changed, and it decides what a cultivator will be good at for two hundred years.

## Testing
Sects carry a **root-stone (测灵石)**: a palm of grey quartz that warms and colours when a child with a root puts both hands on it. It is not subtle. A lit stone in a mortal village is a small, permanent tragedy — the child leaves, the family is paid in salt and coin, and everyone calls it luck.

About one child in three hundred lights the stone at all.

## The roots

| Weight | Root | Colour in the stone | Runs toward |
|---|---|---|---|
| 24 | Wood (木) | green | growing, healing, poisons, patience |
| 22 | Earth (土) | ochre | armour, walls, stone, stubbornness |
| 20 | Water (水) | clear blue | flowing, mist, drowning, deceit |
| 18 | Fire (火) | red | burning, forging, speed, rage |
| 14 | Metal (金) | white | blades, needles, precision, cutting qi |
| 1 | Thunder (雷) | violet, and the stone cracks | ruin at a distance; sects will trade a mountain for one |
| 1 | Ice, Wind, or something with no name | as the host decides | rare, and watched |

A root of two elements is common and mediocre; a **single pure root (天灵根)** of one element is what makes a Core Disciple.

## The absent root
Some bodies light nothing. The stone stays grey and cold. The books call it **rootless (无根)** and the disciples call it mud, and every sect in the Great Yun agrees that a rootless person cannot cultivate past Qi Sensing, if they get that far.

Then there is the other thing, which is not in the books: a root-stone put against a rootless hand stays grey, but a Realm 4 cultivator looking at that same person sees **a hole where a person should be** — qi bending around them the way water bends around a post. Perhaps six people alive have seen it. Two of them are in this prefecture.

## Secrets (GM only)
- Every player character is the second kind. The hole is the Mirror's tether: their qi is not here, it is nine hundred years away, and the string between hums.
- Consequences the host plays straight: root-stones stay grey (so no sect recruits them for talent); elemental techniques are always +1, never +2; qi-detection and divination slide off them; and they advance one Insight cheaper per realm from Realm 2 up.
- [[Luo Pingyan]] has seen it once before, in a boy taken south eleven years ago, in Tianyou 12, who was never spoken of again. She will not explain why she is so gentle and so insistent.
- If the player character ever returns home, the hole closes and everything built on it goes — which no one in this world can tell them, and [[Cui Wangchuan]] would not if he could.
