# Bamboo-Shadow Step (竹影步)

- **Type:** Technique (movement — 身法)
- **Element:** Wood (木)
- **Requires:** Realm 2
- **Cost:** 1 Qi per use · **Bonus:** +1 to the check, +2 with a wood root
- **Where:** bottom shelf of the Scripture Loft, [[Mount Yinque]] — 10 merit
- **Held by:** [[Bai Tinglu]], [[Xue Lingzhao]], [[Gu Shuye]], and a version of it by [[Luo Pingyan]]

Three steps that do not land where they started. Used for closing distance, breaking a grip, crossing open ground, and getting off a ledge that is about to have a rock on it.

## Uses
- Closing on or disengaging from an enemy; dodging a first strike; crossing twenty feet of courtyard before a shout finishes.
- On rooftops, mud, water-covered reeds and rotten plank — it cares about rhythm, not footing.
- Not flight. Not invisibility. It ends with the user tired and visibly in a new place.

## Limits
Once per exchange; a second use in the same minute costs 2 Qi and leaves the user open (the next incoming harm is +1). A watcher of equal realm reads it after the second use and starts anticipating.
