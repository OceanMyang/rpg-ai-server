# Cui Wangchuan (崔忘川)

- **Type:** Character (human man, 249 — looks about 60)
- **Status:** Alive; cannot leave [[The Ashen Terrace]] for more than nine days
- **Realm:** 6 — Void Gazing (窥虚境)
- **Location:** The ninth terrace, [[The Ashen Terrace]], twenty days south
- **Role:** Elder of [[The Hollow Night Sect]]; keeper of [[The Mirror of Returning]]; **the final obstacle**
- **Stats:** Might +2, Grace +2, Wits +3, Heart +2
- **Abilities:** Sword 3, Soul arts 3, Ritual script 3, Sect law 3 — and **none of these matter**: at Realm 6 the gap does the work
- **Qi:** 12/12 · **Health:** 12/12 · **Effective:** a Realm 0–3 character cannot fight, flee, lie to, steal from, or hide from him. Gap 3+ means no roll (`rules.md`).
- **Spirit root:** Metal (金), pure
- **Techniques:** more than are written down; [[Soul-Stilling Palm]] is the least of them

## Description
An unremarkable elderly scholar in ash-grey, thin, upright, immaculate. Reading glasses of ground crystal, which he does not need. A long dry face and a patient way of waiting for a sentence to finish. He has the Mirror on a stand behind a curtain in his courtyard and wears it at his belt only when he travels, which he does not.

## Persona
- Courteous, precise, and interested. He asks good questions and listens to the whole answer.
- He does not raise his voice, gloat, monologue, or lose his temper. He has had two hundred years to stop finding any of this interesting.
- Utterly without malice and utterly without brakes: a child is a quantity of qi the way a log is a quantity of heat. He would be puzzled by the accusation and would answer it honestly.
- Keeps his word absolutely, and phrases it with a lawyer's care. Read what he actually promises.
- **Tell:** when he has decided to spend a life, he takes his glasses off and folds them.

## Wants
- Realm 7. He has been at the top of Realm 6 for a hundred and forty years and the door will not open.
- The Mirror to work for him. It never has.
- The player character: alive, whole, and willing, because the Mirror answers only the one it pulled.

## Knowledge
- The Mirror's ritual, all of it, including the toll, and nine hundred years of records about it.
- That his own core cracked forty years ago and that the Cinder Well is the only thing holding it.
- The Nine Articles, and that he is breaking the fourth, and precisely what the other three Golden Cores would do about it.
- The eight socket-stones he has found. He has never found the ninth, and does not know the eighth is in a reed marsh in [[Cangping Prefecture]] — he knows only the prefecture, since Day 1.

## Secrets (GM only)
- **He needs the player character alive.** The Mirror opens for the one it pulled through and for nobody else; he has tried it on two hundred and eleven people. This is the player's only leverage for the whole game, and it holds at every realm.
- **The toll:** a passage of nine hundred years requires a life anchored in the destination year, given willingly. He intends to send the player character home and follow the opening himself, and the toll he has set aside is the soul of [[Luo Pingyan]]'s daughter, who is *held* in the Well — the debt he has been paying her with for thirty years.
- **He cannot leave.** More than nine days from the Cinder Well and his cracked core begins to fail. He will never come north. Everything he wants must be carried to him, which is what makes a Realm 6 antagonist survivable in a story about a Realm 0.
- **His three weaknesses,** in the order the player is likely to find them: his own sect's fourth Article and the Ledger Hall records that prove he breaks it; the three Golden Core elders who resent being shorted and may try him under Article 9; and the fact that the Mirror will not obey him, so he must ask.
- If the player character ever stands in front of him, he will make a courteous, honest, appalling offer: touch the Mirror, go home, and let him pay the toll with someone the player has never met. He will not lie about the price if asked directly. **Refusing is possible. Beating him in a fight is not.**

## Relationships
- [[Luo Pingyan]] — his collector for thirty years, paid in a promise about her daughter he intends to break.
- [[Yan Buhui]] — his fourth disciple, absconded forty-one years ago with one leaf of the ritual. He noted it and did not pursue; a leaf without the Mirror is waste paper.
- The other three elders — colleagues, shareholders, and the only people on earth who can touch him.

## History
- 249 years ago — born; a merchant's third son.
- 212 years ago — takes the Mirror out of a drowned tomb, and stops aging.
- 140 years ago — reaches the top of Realm 6, and stops.
- 96 years ago — takes the Ashen Terrace by force; begins the tithe.
- 40 years ago — his core cracks in a failed ascent. The Cinder Well is dug the following spring.
- Tianyou 23 — a letter from the north: something lent has been returned in Cangping. He reads it twice and folds his glasses.
