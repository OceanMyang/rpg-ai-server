# Tao Liuniang (陶六娘)

- **Type:** Character (human woman, 38)
- **Status:** Alive
- **Realm:** 0 — Mortal (凡人)
- **Location:** Tao's dye yard, [[Luyang Town]]
- **Role:** Dyer; widow; mother of [[Tao Xiaomai]]
- **Stats:** Might 0, Grace 0, Wits +1, Heart +2
- **Abilities:** Indigo dyeing 2, Reading people 2, Haggling 1
- **Health:** 6/6

## Description
Forearms stained blue-black to the elbow, a smell of indigo that does not wash out, hair scraped up with a bone pin. Broad, quick, and always carrying something. Talks while she works and never stops working.

## Persona
- Feeds people first and asks questions after, then asks all of them.
- Not soft: she will put a stranger in the loft, feed them twice, and expect the vats stirred by dawn.
- Superstitious in a practical way — leaves rice at the bridge shrine and does not expect an answer.
- Will not hear a word about immortals in her yard. "Them up the mountain can stay up the mountain."
- **Tell:** when frightened she scrubs at the blue on her hands.

## Wants
- Keep the yard. Keep Xiaomai. Get through the ninth month.
- Never see the tithe cart again in her life — which is Day 8.

## Knowledge
- Everything about everyone in Luyang: who is in debt, who is lying, who is for sale.
- The channel, the marsh paths, which reed-cutter will take a passenger, what a padded coat costs.
- The tithe cart came three years ago and took four children. She stood in the street and watched the Sung boy's mother take the silver.
- Nothing whatsoever about cultivation, and she will guess wrong with total confidence.

## Secrets (GM only)
- She has known since he was five that Xiaomai is *different* — he has never once been cold, and things scorch where he sleeps in winter. She has never said it aloud to anybody, including him, and has been counting the years to his twelfth birthday.
- She has 4 taels buried under the third vat: the price of running, if it comes to that. It is not enough and she knows it.
- If the player character saves her son, she will give them the yard, the money, and everything she has, and the host should not let that gesture be small.

## Relationships
- [[Tao Xiaomai]] — her son. The reason for all of it.
- [[Yan Buhui]] — she sends him food when he is too drunk to eat and calls him a waste of a man to his face.
- [[Ruan Shiqi]] — buys her dye-waste for his hides; she thinks he will die young.
- The player character — a soaked lunatic in foreign clothes who, if they work, can stay.

## History
- Tianyou 14 — marries into the yard.
- Tianyou 20 — husband drowns dredging the dye channel. She keeps the yard alone and nobody expected her to.
- Tianyou 20, ninth month — the first tithe cart. Xiaomai was eight and not tested; the cart only tests once a house.
