# Cultivation Realms (境界)

- **Type:** Lore — the ladder every living thing stands on
- **Mechanics:** see `rules.md` for gaps, Insight, thresholds and breakthrough

What cultivators call the road (道). Realm is not a level on a character sheet to the people in this world; it is the difference between a man and a thing that looks like a man.

## The ladder

| Realm | Name | What it means, seen from outside |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Mortal (凡人) | Everyone you have ever met. Dies of a cut that goes bad. |
| 1 | Qi Sensing (引气境) | Feels qi in the air like a draught under a door. Sleeps four hours, never gets winded, heals fast. Can hold one technique. No one is impressed. |
| 2 | Qi Refining (炼气境) | Moves qi through the body. Beats three strong men. Jumps a river-cart. Bare hands and sticks stop mattering. The rank and file of every sect. |
| 3 | Foundation Building (筑基境) | Has built a foundation that will hold a core. Can go nine days without food, walk across mud without sinking, take a knife and keep going. A village's idea of an immortal. |
| 4 | Golden Core (金丹境) | A core of condensed qi in the belly. Lives 200 years. Kills a Foundation cultivator with a gesture and a room of mortals without deciding to. Sect heads, elders, the people who own things. |
| 5 | Nascent Soul (元婴境) | Soul made separable. Survives the death of the body, briefly. Four or five in the whole southern region, and they do not travel for small reasons. |
| 6 | Void Gazing (窥虚境) | Reads the seams of the world — space, breath, the edge of time. Hears a lie from across a courtyard. Perhaps eleven alive. [[Cui Wangchuan]] is one. |
| 7 | Ascendant (登仙) | Left. Stories, tablets, and empty mountaintop shrines. |

## How ordinary people read it
- Nobody says "Realm 3". They say *that one's built a foundation*, or *he's got a core*, or, of anything above that, *an immortal (仙人)* — and get off the road.
- Realm is visible at a glance to anyone Realm 2 or above and invisible to mortals, who go by clothes, sword, and how wet the person is in the rain.
- A cultivator whose realm cannot be read at all is either far above the looker, or something wrong. The player character reads as *nothing* — not empty, not weak: absent. See [[Spirit Roots]].

## Secrets (GM only)
- The thresholds in `rules.md` are the honest ladder. The player character's climb is discounted by one Insight per realm after the first, and neither they nor anyone else knows why. The Mirror is holding a place for them.
- [[Cui Wangchuan]] has stood at Realm 6 for a hundred and forty years and cannot advance. He believes the Mirror is the stair to Realm 7. He is wrong about what it is for, and right that it is the only way.
