# Fennick

- **Type:** Character (human man, 35)
- **Status:** Alive
- **Location:** Camped with his wagon in the yard behind [[The Thirsty Heron]], [[Dunmere]]
- **Role:** Traveling peddler — lately, mostly a water-seller
- **Abilities:** Haggling 2, Sleight of hand 1, Running away 2

## Description
Wiry and sharp-featured, a feathered hat, a gold tooth, and a smile that arrives a moment before his eyes agree to it. His mule, Duchess, is better groomed than he is. His wagon carries water barrels hauled over the hills, plus pots, needles, rope, lamp oil, and "genuine relics".

## Persona
- Charming, slippery, forever selling. Calls everyone "friend".
- Cowardly, but not cruel. Gouges prices, but keeps any bargain he's been paid for.
- Knows the village hates him for his water prices and pretends he doesn't care.

## Knowledge
- The roads and prices of the whole region; he comes through Merrowdale every few weeks.

## Secrets (GM only)
- **He's a witness.** The night before Midsummer's Eve he was camped beside the shrine road and saw a tall woman in a veil carry a heavy sack from the shrine's bell-house to the cottage behind it. He thought nothing of it — until he heard the bell had been "stolen".
- He's afraid to get involved: Caddock might call him a witch's accomplice. He'll talk for a price (2 silver, a good trade, or a Heart roll), or if frightened (a Might roll to intimidate) — but insists, "You never heard it from me."

## Relationships
- [[Agna Holt]] — his best customer; they dislike each other.
- [[Brother Caddock]] — Fennick keeps out of his way.

## History
- The night before Midsummer's Eve, 1147 — camped on the shrine road; saw the veiled woman.
- 2 days before Day 1 — returns to Dunmere with a load of water barrels.
