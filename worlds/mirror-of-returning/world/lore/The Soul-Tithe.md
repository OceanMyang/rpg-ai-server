# The Soul-Tithe (魂税)

- **Type:** Lore — the crime everyone in the south has agreed to live with
- **Run by:** [[The Hollow Night Sect]], collected in [[Cangping Prefecture]] by [[Luo Pingyan]]

Once a year, after the harvest tax, a second cart comes. Two men, a woman with a ledger, a pair of [[Ash Hounds]], and a **root-stone (测灵石)**. Every child under twelve in the village puts both hands on the stone. The ones who light it leave on the cart.

## How it is dressed up
It is called an apprenticeship, and it is paid: three taels of silver, a bolt of good cloth, and a sealed **tithe tally (魂契牌)** promising the family a visit every third year. The papers are correct. The woman is kind, remembers names, and brings sweets. Villages that have lost children argue afterwards about whether it was a blessing.

No child has ever come back, and no third-year visit has ever been made.

## What actually happens
The children are taken south to [[The Ashen Terrace]]. Those with strong roots are raised as Hollow Night disciples, and some live well. The rest are **vessels**: the sect's elders burn a child's untrained qi to hold a wound closed, fill a talisman, or keep an old cultivator's cracked core from failing. It takes nine days and the child does not wake up on the ninth.

## Why nobody stops it
- The Empire does not enter sect business — see [[The Great Yun Dynasty]].
- The nearest sect that might object is [[The Green Bamboo Sect]]: nineteen disciples, one Golden Core, against a sect with four Golden Cores and a Void Gazing elder. [[Gu Shuye]] has done the arithmetic every year for eight years.
- Villagers who hid a child three years ago, in Tianyou 20, lost the child, the house, and two uncles.

## What it costs to interfere
Everything, and the host should make that plain in advance through what the characters say — then let the player do it anyway. Hiding a child, faking a root-stone reading, buying a tally back, killing a hound, or getting the sect to send a witness are all possible at Realm 1 with a plan. Killing Luo Pingyan is not.

## Secrets (GM only)
- The tithe exists because of [[Cui Wangchuan]]'s cracked core. Four of every five vessels are burned for him alone; the rest of the sect gets the leavings and resents it.
- Luo Pingyan keeps her own ledger of names, in a hand nobody else reads, of the children she sent to be burned. She has never let herself count them.
- [[Tao Xiaomai]] will light the stone red on Day 8.
