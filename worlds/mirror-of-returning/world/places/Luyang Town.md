# Luyang Town (芦阳镇)

- **Type:** Place (market town, about 700 people)
- **In:** [[Cangping Prefecture]]
- **Status:** Wet, broke, and quietly dreading the ninth month

## Overview
One street of packed mud along the channel, a stone bridge with a shrine-niche at each end, and behind the street the dye yards: sunken vats of indigo that make the whole town smell of piss and iron. Reed-paper drying frames on every roof. Eleven buildings that matter, and everyone knows which door you came out of.

No walls. No garrison — two of the magistrate's runners come through every fortnight. If something with qi in it walks down the street, the town's entire plan is to look at the floor.

## Where things are
- **Tao's dye yard (陶家染坊)** — south end, four vats and a drying loft. [[Tao Liuniang]] and her son [[Tao Xiaomai]]. The loft is dry, and the likeliest first roof over the PC's head.
- **The Broken Oar (断桨居)** — the only inn: eight mats, one room, rice wine cut with water. 30 coin a night, 5 for a bowl of noodles. [[Yan Buhui]] drinks in the corner by the door and has not paid since the summer.
- **Bridge-end market** — mornings only. Eels, reed mats, salt, a barber who pulls teeth, a pawnbroker named Old Kuai who will lend 60 coin on anything shiny and ask no questions he cannot profit from.
- **The reed-paper sheds** — work for anyone with hands: 12 coin a day, dawn to dusk, and a foreman who will hire a stranger without a surname.
- **Ruan's shack** — north of the bridge, hides on the frames outside. [[Ruan Shiqi]] posts the bounties nobody at the sect will take.
- **The shrine-niches** — a cracked stone official of the waters at each bridge-end. People leave rice. Nothing answers.

## Money here
A day's labour 12 coin. Noodles 5. A night's lodging 30. A padded coat 90. A used sword 1 tael (1,000 coin) and the pawnbroker has exactly one. Nobody in this town has ever held a spirit stone.

## What the town will do about a stranger
They will feed a soaked, empty-handed stranger once — this is a place where the channel drowns people — and then want to know their surname, their county, and who vouches for them. No answer means the loft, not the inn, and a foreman's job at best. Modern clothes get called foreign, then get stolen, then get seen on someone else's back.

## History
- Tianyou 20 — four children taken by the tithe cart. The town has not agreed about it since.
- Tianyou 23, ninth month, Day 1 — a stranger in impossible clothes comes up the south path out of the rain.
