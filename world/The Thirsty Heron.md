# The Thirsty Heron

- **Type:** Location (inn and tavern)
- **Status:** Open; business poor; ale running short
- **Location:** On the green in [[Dunmere]]
- **Owner:** [[Agna Holt]]

## Description
A long, low, timber-framed inn under a sagging thatch, its signboard a heron with its beak gaping open — a village joke that stopped being funny around the fortieth dry day. Inside: smoke-blackened beams, a long scarred bar, benches, a cold hearth (too hot to light it), and three rooms upstairs. [[Brother Caddock]] has the best one. [[Fennick]]'s wagon is parked in the yard out back.

## Services
Ale 4c · bean stew and bread 5c · a bed 5c a night · a bath: "Are you mad? There's a drought."

## Regulars
Old Pell, the Dowd twins, and Garrow the smith (see [[Dunmere]]). In the evening, after Caddock's sermon, the room fills with his listeners.
