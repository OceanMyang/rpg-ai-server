# Blackwater Gorge (墨水峡)

- **Type:** Place (gorge and road)
- **Where:** 3 hours north-west of [[Luyang Town]], where the channel cuts through the clay hills
- **Status:** The only road south for a cart

## Overview
Ninety feet of black rock on both sides, water the colour of ink, and a ledge road wide enough for one cart cut into the eastern wall. Above the ledge, a rope-and-plank bridge nobody maintains. In the ninth month the water is high and loud enough that two people on the ledge cannot hear a third.

Everything on wheels going south goes through here, including the tithe cart on Day 9, and the hills either side belong to nobody.

## Features
- **The ledge** — one cart wide, no rail, a 40-foot drop. A cart that stops here cannot turn around.
- **The rope bridge** — reachable by a goat path, 60 feet up, and it overlooks the ledge's narrowest point. Anyone who has ever thought about physics can see what could be dropped from it.
- **The eel weirs** — wicker cages in the slack water below, tended by two brothers from Luyang who see everything that passes and are for sale cheap.
- **The ink-snake pools** — slack water at the north end where [[Ink Serpents]] breed. Locals know to keep a stick in the water ahead of them.
- **The old toll house** — roofless, two walls, a hearth that still draws. Sleeps four out of the rain and is the obvious ambush site, which means the obvious place to be ambushed.

## Why it matters
This is where a Realm 1 with a plan can beat a Realm 4 — not in a fight, but by choosing the ground. Rockfall, a cut rope, a frightened horse on a one-cart ledge, a cart that cannot turn. The host should let geography do what dice cannot, and should still make the cost real: a Golden Core cultivator who survives a rockfall knows exactly who arranged it.

## History
- Cut by prefecture labour 140 years ago; eleven men died and the magistrate's stone at the south end says nine.
- Tianyou 20, 21, 22 — the tithe cart passed through unmolested each ninth month.
