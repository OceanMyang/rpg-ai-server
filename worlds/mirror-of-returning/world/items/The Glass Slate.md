# The Glass Slate (琉璃板)

- **Type:** Item (the player character's phone, as this world sees it)
- **Status:** In the player character's pocket. Battery at whatever it was when they fell — call it a fifth, and dying.
- **Value:** to a pawnbroker, 2 taels for the glass and the strange metal; to [[Luo Pingyan]], a reason to be certain

Black glass in a metal frame, cold, too smooth, too flat. Touched, it lights up from inside with no flame and no heat and shows pictures of impossible rooms. Nobody in [[The Great Yun Dynasty]] has seen glass like this, or light like this, and no cultivator can find a thread of qi in it, which is worse.

## What it can actually do
- Light: a torch that does not gutter, for a handful of hours total.
- A mirror, a lens, a signal flashed across [[Blackwater Gorge]] at night.
- Pictures: photographs of people and places that cannot exist. This is the single most convincing object the player character owns, and the most dangerous to show.
- Arithmetic, a clock, a compass if it has one, and whatever is already stored on it — which the host should decide once, concretely, and stick to.

## What it cannot do
Recharge. There is no electricity in the ninth month of Tianyou 23. Every use spends a finite thing, and the host should count it out loud: *a fifth, a tenth, a sliver, nothing.* After that it is a beautiful black rectangle of glass.

## How people react
- Mortals: witchcraft, a demon-mirror, a god's tablet. [[Tao Liuniang]] will tell them to get it out of her yard. [[Tao Xiaomai]] will tell the whole street.
- Cultivators: an unfamiliar treasure with no qi in it, which is impossible, which means it is either mortal rubbish or something far above their reading. Most assume the second and become very careful.
- Old Kuai the pawnbroker will pay 2 taels and report it to the first person who asks.
- [[Luo Pingyan]]: she will ask to hold it, and she will already know.

## Secrets (GM only)
- Showing it to anyone outside the Tao household advances **The Wake in the Mirror** clock by a day. Pawning it advances it by two.
- It is not a treasure and never becomes one. It has no qi, it cannot be cultivated into anything, and no amount of clever play makes it a weapon. Its only real power is proof — and proof is what makes two people eventually believe the player character.
