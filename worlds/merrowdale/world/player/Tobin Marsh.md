# Tobin Marsh

- **Type:** Character (player character — human man, 26; he/him)
- **Status:** Alive
- **Location:** On the hill road, about to walk down into [[Dunmere]]
- **Concept:** A junior scholar of the Royal Archive, sent west to copy the records of old country shrines before the Church's witch-finders burn them
- **Stats:** Might −1, Grace 0, Wits +2, Heart +1
- **Knack:** Old scripts and dusty records (+1 when it applies: reading old writing, digging through documents, knowing obscure history)
- **Health:** 6/6
- **Conditions:** none
- **Silver:** 10 silver, 0 copper

## Description
Thin and long-limbed, with wire spectacles mended with thread, ink ground into the creases of his fingers, and a sunburnt nose. Road dust from Kettle Cross covers him to the knees. He talks a little too fast when he's interested in something, which is often.

## Inventory
- Satchel holding a writing case: quills, a pot of ink, a penknife, a sheaf of good paper, and charcoal sticks for taking rubbings
- A letter of commission from the Royal Archive, under the Archivist's seal, requesting "all courtesy and access to records" (it carries no legal force)
- A stout ash walking staff
- A waterskin, about half full
- Travel clothes, a bedroll, a spare shirt

## Knows
- The Archive's catalogue lists "Merrowdale — a shrine to a lake-spirit, with ledgers said to go back three centuries." His task is to copy them.
- At Kettle Cross he read the reeve's notice: **20 silver** to whoever returns the Rain Bell of Dunmere.
- The Church of the Dawn calls the worship of local spirits demon-worship. Its witch-finders have burned shrine records in other provinces.
