# Reed-Breath Meditation (芦息诀)

- **Type:** Technique (foundation method — 引气法)
- **Element:** none; works for any root and for no root
- **Rank:** lowest. Every sect in the south has a version and none of them boast about it.
- **Cost:** none to hold. It is not used in a moment; it is how a cultivator sleeps.
- **Taught by:** [[The Green Bamboo Sect]] on promotion to outer disciple · [[Ruan Shiqi]], badly, to anyone who asks twice and works a full day first · [[Yan Buhui]], properly, to someone he decides to trust

The first rung. Six hours seated, breathing on a count that matches the reeds in wind — four in, hold, six out — drawing the thinnest available qi in through the soles of the feet and the base of the spine. It is dull, it hurts in the knees and shoulders, and it is the difference between a person and a cultivator.

## What it does
- **Realm 0 → 1.** Holding this technique and meditating correctly is how Insight is earned at all: a night of it at a qi-rich place is +1 Insight (once per place per realm).
- At Realm 1 and above it is how Qi refills overnight.
- Without it, a person can gather Insight from missions, revelations and pills, but cannot break through to Realm 1 at all. **This is the gate, and it is the player character's first real objective.**

## Learning it
From a willing teacher: one night of instruction and it takes. From a copied page without a teacher: a `Wits` check after three days of practice; on 7–9 it works but every breakthrough is at −1 forever (the habit is wrong and cannot be unlearned); on 6 or less, a qi deviation and 1 harm.

Ruan Shiqi teaches the copied-page version, because that is what he has. Yan Buhui can teach the real one, and can correct a wrong habit in one very unpleasant night.

## Tells
A cultivator who has this can be recognised by another cultivator at a glance: the breath, the stillness, the way they sit down. Mortals see somebody sitting in the cold for no reason.
