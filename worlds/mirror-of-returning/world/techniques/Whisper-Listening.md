# Whisper-Listening (听隙)

- **Type:** Technique (perception — 感知)
- **Element:** Water (水)
- **Requires:** Realm 1
- **Cost:** 1 Qi per use · **Bonus:** +1 to the check, +2 with a water root
- **Held by:** [[Yan Buhui]], [[Luo Pingyan]]
- **Taught by:** Yan Buhui only, and only to someone he trusts — it is a Hollow Night technique and he knows exactly what he is handing over

Listening to the gap (隙) rather than the sound: the half-beat before a lie, the draught of qi under a door, the difference between an empty room and a room where somebody is holding their breath.

## Uses
- Noticing qi: whether a person is a cultivator, roughly what realm (within one), whether a place has a vein under it.
- Hearing a lie — **not its content**. The user knows the sentence was false and learns nothing about what is true.
- Hearing through a wall, finding what is behind a curtain, counting people in the dark.

## Limits
- A user of higher realm notices they are being listened to, and takes it as an insult or a threat. Using this on [[Luo Pingyan]] tells her more than it tells the user.
- It slides off a player character entirely — and off anyone else who came through the Mirror. So does every other divination.
- Two uses in an hour bring on a headache and −1 Wits for the day.

## Why it matters
It is the investigation technique. Half of this story is finding out who is lying to the player character — [[Jiang Sui'an]] first of all — and this is the tool that makes a Realm 1 dangerous in a room full of their betters.
