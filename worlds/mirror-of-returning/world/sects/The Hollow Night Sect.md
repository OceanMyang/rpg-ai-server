# The Hollow Night Sect (玄冥宗)

- **Type:** Sect (major, first rate in the south)
- **Seat:** [[The Ashen Terrace]], twenty days south
- **Strength:** ~400 disciples, 4 Golden Core elders, 1 Void Gazing elder — [[Cui Wangchuan]]
- **In this prefecture:** [[Luo Pingyan]], her ledger, two hound-handlers, and the [[The Soul-Tithe]]

The antagonist sect, and the reason the ladder matters. They are not cackling villains; they are an institution, and institutions are worse.

## What they believe
That qi is a substance, not a virtue. That a life too small to cultivate is material — grain grows to be eaten, and a rootless child in a reed village is not wasted when it is spent on someone who will last two hundred years. They teach this calmly, in writing, to fourteen-year-olds, and most of them come to agree.

## How they behave
- Correct, patient, and papered. Every child bought comes with a contract and three taels. Every debt is honoured. They keep their word, which is what makes them hard to fight — nobody in the prefecture can point to a broken promise.
- They do not raid, burn or threaten where a purchase will do. They do not need to.
- They punish interference precisely and once: the house, the uncles, no more. The point is the lesson, not the cruelty.

## The Nine Articles (九律)
Their own law, carved at their first gate, and genuinely enforced — this is the seam in them:
1. A disciple's life belongs to the sect. ... 4. **No elder shall spend the sect's substance upon his own body.** ... 7. A tithe taken must be recorded in the Ledger Hall. 9. An elder may be tried by three elders.

## Who the player will actually meet
- **[[Luo Pingyan]]** — Realm 4, the tithe collector, and a Golden Core is the end of the world at Realm 0–2. She is kind. She will offer the player character food, money, an apprenticeship, and a way home, and three of those are true.
- **Hound-handlers** — Realm 2, black with ash-grey collars, always in pairs, always with [[Ash Hounds]].
- **[[Cui Wangchuan]]** — a rumour, then a letter, then a voice. Not in person for a very long time.

## Secrets (GM only)
- Article 4 is being broken every year, by the elder who wrote the enforcement clause. Four of every five tithed children are burned in the Cinder Well to keep his cracked core from failing. It is recorded in the Ledger Hall, in full, because Article 7 required it and nobody imagined anyone reading both.
- The other three Golden Core elders know they are being shorted and do not know why. They would try him tomorrow if they had proof.
- Luo Pingyan has proof and has never used it. She is not loyal to him; she is afraid of him, and she is owed something no one else can pay.
