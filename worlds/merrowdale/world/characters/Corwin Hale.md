# Corwin Hale

- **Type:** Character (human man, 52)
- **Status:** Alive
- **Location:** [[Dunmere]] — the reeve's house beside the green; at dawn, handing out water rations at the well
- **Role:** Reeve of Dunmere — elected headman, answerable to [[Lord Vance]] for taxes and order
- **Abilities:** Organizing folk 2, Haggling 1, Cudgel 1

## Description
Broad and balding, farmer's hands, sunburnt neck, a ledger always under his arm. His voice is hoarse from shouting in the heat.

## Persona
- Practical, blunt, overworked. Cares about the village as a whole more than any one person in it.
- Short with idlers and with strangers who "come to gawk at our misery", but fair to anyone who offers real help.
- Quietly superstitious: touches the doorframe for luck.

## Wants
- Rain. Failing that: keep order, keep families from leaving, and keep Lord Vance from evicting anyone over the harvest tax.

## Knowledge
- Rations: one bucket per household per day from the village well, and the well is nearly dry.
- He posted the reward notice at Kettle Cross: **20 silver**, collected from the whole village, for the return of the Rain Bell. He will honor it.
- He wrote to the Church for help; Brother Caddock came.
- Lord Vance sells water from the deep well at Vance Hall for 1 silver a barrel — more than most families can pay.

## Secrets (GM only)
- He has been secretly drawing an extra bucket at night for his wife, Ada, who is pregnant and unwell. He is ashamed of it.
- He is starting to fear Caddock's mob more than the drought. With evidence against the witch-hunt he would turn — but he won't stand against the mob alone.
- As reeve, his sworn oath binds the village in matters of custom. That matters for any new pact with [[The Lady of the Mere]].

## Relationships
- [[Brother Caddock]] — invited him; now uneasy about him.
- [[Sister Maren]] — respects her; begs her for a miracle.
- [[Agna Holt]] — old friend; she tells him hard truths.
- [[Lord Vance]] — his liege; Corwin fears him.

## History
- 1138 — elected reeve.
- 10 days before Day 1 — writes to the Church and posts the reward notice at Kettle Cross.
- 3 days before Day 1 — Brother Caddock arrives.
