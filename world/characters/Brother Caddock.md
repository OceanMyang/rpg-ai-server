# Brother Caddock

- **Type:** Character (human man, 38)
- **Status:** Alive
- **Location:** [[Dunmere]] — lodging at [[The Thirsty Heron]]; preaches on the green at dawn and dusk; spends the day questioning villagers
- **Role:** Witch-finder of the Church of the Dawn (see [[Aldermark]]), sent at the reeve's request
- **Abilities:** Preaching and rousing crowds 2, Scripture 2, Iron mace 1

## Description
Gaunt and shaven-headed, his scalp sunburnt, his eyes pale and fervent. A stained white robe with a gold sunburst; a heavy iron mace at his belt. His voice is startlingly deep and carries across the whole green.

## Persona
- Utterly sincere. He believes the Lady is a demon and the old ways are chains, and that he is saving these people.
- Charismatic and relentless; courteous to the faithful, cold to "heretics". Never takes a bribe.
- Can be argued with, but only on his own ground — scripture, evidence, courage. Persuading him is at −2; evidence he can see with his own eyes helps.
- Beneath it all, desperate: he has seen what drought does to children.

## Wants
- End the drought by cleansing the valley: burn the witch, topple the Bellstone, and consecrate the lake to the Dawn.

## Plans (see Clocks in `game/state.md`)
- Days 1–3: preach at dawn and dusk; question villagers about the witch; gather followers.
- Evening of Day 4: lead a mob of about 20 into [[Thornwood]] to seize [[Granny Nettle]].
- Dusk of Day 5: burn her on the green, then march on the [[Mere Shrine]] and topple the Bellstone.

## Knowledge
- Church doctrine on "hedge-spirits". He knows little of the Lady's actual lore and scorns it.
- Villagers have told him the witch in the wood "was sweet on the Ashby boy the lake took" — to him, proof she trucks with the lake-demon.

## Secrets (GM only)
- He grew up in a famine in the eastern provinces; his little brother starved to death. He cannot bear to watch it happen again.
- If shown undeniable proof that the drought can end another way — above all, rain actually returning — he relents: shaken, but honest enough to admit it.

## Relationships
- [[Corwin Hale]] — sent for him, and is now having second thoughts.
- [[Granny Nettle]] — "the witch"; his target.
- [[Sister Maren]] — "a misguided soul" he would like to see recant.
- [[Agna Holt]] — his reluctant landlady.
- [[Lord Vance]] — gave him the writ to hunt witches in Merrowdale.

## History
- 3 days before Day 1 — arrives in Dunmere bearing a writ from Lord Vance.
