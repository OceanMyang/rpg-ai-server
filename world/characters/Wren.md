# Wren

- **Type:** Character (human girl, 12)
- **Status:** Alive
- **Location:** [[Mere Shrine]]; often out on the mudflats of [[Merrow Lake]], hunting for things the water has uncovered
- **Role:** Acolyte of the Mere Shrine — and, by custom, its bell-ringer
- **Abilities:** Sneaking 1, Knowing every path in Dunmere and across the mudflats 2

## Description
Small, freckled, and sun-browned, with a mop of dark hair and a gap in her front teeth. Muddy to the knees most days. Carries a satchel of "treasures": a green glass bead, an old copper coin, and a big rusted iron key she dug out of the mud.

## Persona
- Bright, fearless, and talkative. Asks strangers endless questions ("Have you seen the sea? Is it really salty?").
- Adores her aunt Maren. She's noticed Maren is sad and secretive, and thinks it's because of the drought.
- Loyal; keeps promises; hates being treated like a baby.
- Dislikes [[Brother Caddock]] ("He smells like candle smoke and he shouted at Aunt Maren").

## Knowledge
- She was meant to ring the Rain Bell this Midsummer and was proud of it. She doesn't know the cost.
- She found the rusted key in the mud near the rooftops of [[Old Wendle]]. (It opens the chapel door there — she doesn't know that.)
- The safe paths across the mudflats, and the way to [[Granny Nettle]]'s hut in [[Thornwood]].

## Secrets (GM only)
- For about a month she has dreamed every night of a young man singing under green water, calling her "the next one". She thinks he is beautiful and sad. It is [[Tam Ashby]]. She hasn't told Maren, so as not to worry her — but she'd tell someone she trusts.
- The call grows stronger every night. On the night of Day 6 she will sleepwalk into the lake (see Clocks in `game/state.md`).
- She secretly visits Granny Nettle, who gives her honey sweets and tells her stories. Maren doesn't know.

## Relationships
- [[Sister Maren]] — aunt and guardian.
- [[Granny Nettle]] — "the witch"; her secret friend.
- [[Agna Holt]] — slips her leftovers from the inn kitchen.

## History
- 1142 — her parents die of fever; Maren takes her in at the shrine.
- Midsummer's Eve, 1147 — the bell is gone and she can't ring it; she cries for a day.
- About 30 days before Day 1 — the dreams begin. Soon after, she finds the key in the mud.
