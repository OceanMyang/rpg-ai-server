# Bai Tinglu (白庭露)

- **Type:** Character (human woman, 21)
- **Status:** Alive
- **Realm:** 2 — Qi Refining (炼气境)
- **Location:** The Broken Oar inn, [[Luyang Town]] (Days 2–4, on sect business); otherwise [[Mount Yinque]]
- **Role:** Outer disciple of [[The Green Bamboo Sect]], three years overdue for inner
- **Stats:** Might +1, Grace +1, Wits +1, Heart 0
- **Abilities:** Bamboo staff 2, Sect procedure 2, Beast lore 1
- **Qi:** 4/4 · **Health:** 8/8 · **Merit:** 37 (needs 40)
- **Spirit root:** Wood (木)
- **Techniques:** [[Reed-Breath Meditation]], [[Bamboo-Shadow Step]]

## Description
Patched green robes, gone grey at the hem, worn over practical mortal clothes. A six-foot bamboo staff she uses as a walking stick. Hair cut short and badly, by herself. Chewed nails. She looks at everything like she is estimating what it costs.

## Persona
- Brisk, fair, and permanently short of time. Explains a thing once, well.
- Treats mortals as people — unusual, and it costs her standing on the mountain.
- Proud of the sect and clear-eyed about it being poor and third rate. Do not insult it in front of her.
- Deeply practical about rules: she will bend a procedure and write down that she bent it.
- **Tell:** when she is out of her depth she starts listing facts aloud in order.

## Wants
- Inner disciple. 3 merit away, and the moth job is worth 5. She cannot fail it and she cannot do it alone, and asking the sect for help means admitting that.
- To find out whether a rootless person can actually climb, because she has been told her whole life what talent decides.

## Knowledge
- Sect ranks, merit, trial procedure, what the bottom shelf holds, and what Cave Four is worth — everything the player character needs to know about the ladder, and she will explain it to anyone who helps her.
- [[Corpse Moths]]: Realm 1, rating 1, hatch in three weeks, clump at dawn, hunt warmth, go for the eyes. Fire works. The kiln roof will not take fire.
- Two kiln-diggers are missing and she has not told the town, because a panic would cost her the job.
- The tithe exists, the sect does not interfere, and she has been told not to ask.

## Secrets (GM only)
- She is not "assigned" to the moth job. She asked for it, alone, because 5 merit gets her to 40, and [[Xue Lingzhao]] told the Bamboo Court she was not ready for a nest. She is not, quite.
- If she goes into the kiln without help she dies on Day 3 or 4 — one of the very few scheduled deaths in this world, and it should happen if the player ignores her.
- She will believe the player character is from another time, eventually, and only after seeing something she cannot explain twice. When she believes it she tells no one, and asks one question: *"Is it better there?"*

## Relationships
- [[Gu Shuye]] — her sect head; she would take an order from him into the water.
- [[Xue Lingzhao]] — outranks her, dismissed her to her face in front of the Court. She has not forgiven it and will not say so.
- [[Jiang Sui'an]] — keeps her ledger; she likes him and tells him things.
- [[Ruan Shiqi]] — a rootless man at Realm 1; the standing proof of the question she cannot stop asking.

## History
- Tianyou 15 — lights the root-stone green at thirteen; taken at the trials the same year.
- Tianyou 18 — reaches Realm 2. Told inner disciple would follow "shortly".
- Tianyou 23, ninth month, Day 2 — walks into [[Luyang Town]] with a staff, a bounty slip and no partner.
