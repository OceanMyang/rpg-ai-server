# Xue Lingzhao (薛凌昭)

- **Type:** Character (human man, 24)
- **Status:** Alive
- **Realm:** 3 — Foundation Building (筑基境)
- **Location:** The Bamboo Court, [[Mount Yinque]]
- **Role:** Core disciple of [[The Green Bamboo Sect]]; its only Foundation disciple; runs the trials on Day 5
- **Stats:** Might +2, Grace +2, Wits 0, Heart −1
- **Abilities:** Sword 3, Sect procedure 2, Contempt 3
- **Qi:** 6/6 · **Health:** 9/9
- **Spirit root:** Metal (金), pure
- **Techniques:** [[Bamboo-Shadow Step]], [[Green Bamboo Spine]], the sect's sword form

## Description
Clean green robes with the cuffs turned, a plain sword he maintains obsessively, a high straight back. Handsome and aware of it. Twenty-four years old and speaks like a man of sixty correcting a child.

## Persona
- Correct before he is kind. He follows the sect's rules exactly, including the ones that protect people he dislikes.
- Believes talent is a moral fact: a lit root-stone means you deserve the mountain, and grey means you are wasting rice. Says so.
- Not a coward and not a liar. If he gives his word he keeps it, which is the crack the player can get into.
- Humiliated by the sect's poverty and would leave if a better sect ever asked.
- **Tell:** when he is impressed against his will he changes the subject.

## Wants
- To be somewhere worthy of him.
- To not be the disciple who watched the Green Bamboo Sect die.
- Approval from [[Gu Shuye]], which he will never admit to wanting and will never get in the form he wants it.

## Knowledge
- Trial procedure, sect law, and the whole top shelf.
- That [[Bai Tinglu]] asked for the moth job alone and that he said she was not ready — in open court, which he considers honesty.
- That a rootless candidate has never made inner disciple in the sect's 420 years. He is right about this.

## Secrets (GM only)
- He said Bai Tinglu was not ready because she **is not**, and because he has read the sect's death register and she is the fourth disciple in his time to take a nest job alone to make up a merit shortfall. He handled it in the worst possible way and he was not wrong.
- He is bound by a sect oath sworn at Foundation: he cannot stand by while another disciple dies in front of him. This is mechanical, not moral — put a disciple in mortal danger in his sight and he will act, whatever he was saying a moment before.
- Beat him at something he respects — not a fight; a plan, a nerve, a piece of knowledge he cannot dismiss — and he becomes a loyal, insufferable ally who will still say the player character has no talent. Convert him and the endgame gains a Foundation sword.
- The Hollow Night has sounded him out once, through [[Jiang Sui'an]], and he did not report it to his sect head. He has not decided why.

## Relationships
- [[Gu Shuye]] — his master; the approval he cannot earn.
- [[Bai Tinglu]] — beneath his notice, and a splinter under his nail.
- [[Jiang Sui'an]] — the steward; he thinks the man is a servant with a key.

## History
- Tianyou 13 — lights the stone white at fourteen; taken.
- Tianyou 21 — Foundation Building at twenty-two, the sect's fastest in ninety years. The sect held a feast it could not afford.
- Tianyou 23, Day 5 — will run the outer-disciple trials, and does it by the letter.
