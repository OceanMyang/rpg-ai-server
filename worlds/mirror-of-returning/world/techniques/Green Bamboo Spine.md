# Green Bamboo Spine (青竹骨)

- **Type:** Technique (body-hardening — 炼体)
- **Element:** Wood (木)
- **Requires:** Realm 2
- **Cost:** 1 Qi, held for one scene · **Bonus:** +1 to endure, +2 with a wood root
- **Where:** second shelf of the Scripture Loft — 25 merit, inner disciples only
- **Held by:** [[Gu Shuye]], [[Xue Lingzhao]]

Qi laid along the bones so they bend instead of breaking. The sect's signature and the reason a poor mountain has survived 420 years: green bamboo disciples are famously hard to finish.

## Effect
- While held: ignore 1 point of incoming harm from any single source (blades and beasts included, not qi attacks from two realms up).
- Falls, crushing, cold and being hit by something large: +1 or +2 with the root.
- Does nothing about poison, drowning, or soul arts — see [[Soul-Stilling Palm]].

## Cost of overuse
Held twice in a day, the user's joints seize that night: −1 to everything the following day. The sect's older disciples all walk slightly wrong.
