# Aldermark

- **Type:** Country (kingdom)
- **Status:** At peace, but its crown is old and distant; remote valleys like [[Merrowdale]] are left to their lords
- **Ruler:** King Osric III — elderly and ailing; the court is run by his chancellor
- **Capital:** Aldmere, twelve days' travel east of Merrowdale
- **Year:** 1147 of the Aldermark reckoning

## Overview
A temperate kingdom of river valleys, alder woods, and small market towns. Lords owe the crown soldiers and taxes; villages owe their lords the same, through elected reeves. Outside the royal highway, roads are rutted tracks.

## Religion
- **The Church of the Dawn** is the kingdom's official faith: one god, the Undying Sun, worshipped at sunrise. Its priests wear white and gold. Its witch-finders hunt hedge-magic and the worship of local spirits, which the Church calls demons.
- In the countryside the old folk-faiths survive quietly — like the worship of [[The Lady of the Mere]] in Merrowdale.

## Law & Customs
- Lords hold justice on their lands; reeves keep order in villages and collect the harvest tax.
- Witchcraft is a crime under Church law, but a witch-finder needs a lord's writ to act on his lands.
- Money: silver pennies ("silver") and copper bits. 10 copper = 1 silver. A laborer earns about 1 silver a week.

## Places
- [[Merrowdale]] — a remote valley in the western hills.

## History
- 1147, late summer — a drought grips Merrowdale; the capital has not noticed.
