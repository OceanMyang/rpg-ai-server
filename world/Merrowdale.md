# Merrowdale

- **Type:** Region (valley)
- **Status:** In the grip of a drought — no rain since Midsummer's Eve (61 days as of Day 1)
- **Part of:** [[Aldermark]], in the western hills
- **Liege:** [[Lord Vance]] of Vance Hall

## Overview
Normally a green bowl of a valley. The River Wend flows in from the northern hills and pools into [[Merrow Lake]], held back at the south end by the Vance Weir, a stone dam built 160 years ago. Now the fields are brown, the Wend is a trickle, and the lake has shrunk from its banks, leaving a wide ring of cracked mud. One road leads out: the hill road east.

## Weather
Roll once per in-game day and record the result in `game/state.md`. Then apply the Active override below.

### Weather Table (late summer)
| Weight | Weather |
|---|---|
| 4 | Clear, hot and dry; the sky a hard, pale blue |
| 3 | Hazy sun and a hot south wind that lifts dust off the fields |
| 2 | Mist off the lake at dawn, burned away by mid-morning |
| 2 | Overcast and heavy; the air thick, still, and sticky |
| 2 | Light rain through the afternoon |
| 1 | A thunderstorm rolls in at evening |
| 1 | Cool, clear, and breezy — a gift of a day |

### Active override: The Drought
While [[The Rain Bell]] goes unrung at the Bellstone, **no rain falls anywhere in Merrowdale**. Reinterpret results:
- *Light rain* → clouds gather all morning, then break apart. Villagers stand in the lanes holding out buckets anyway.
- *Thunderstorm* → **dry thunder**: black clouds boil over the hills, lightning cracks, wind howls — and not one drop falls. Lightning may set the dry grass alight (`oracle unlikely`).
- *Mist* → thin, and it smells of mud and rot.

Beyond the valley's rim, the weather is normal. When the pact is renewed, remove this override: rain falls the following day.

## History
- ~400 years ago — the founders of Wendle make a pact with [[The Lady of the Mere]]; gentle rain ever since.
- ~160 years ago — the Vance Weir raises the lake and drowns [[Old Wendle]].
- Midsummer's Eve, 1147 — [[The Rain Bell]] goes missing. The rain stops.
