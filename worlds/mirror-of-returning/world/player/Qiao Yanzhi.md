- **Type:** Character (player character — human man, 26; he/him)
- **Status:** Alive; soaked, freezing, and one thousand and forty-one years from home

## Description
A rain jacket over a cardigan, cotton gloves in one pocket, cracked glasses. Thin, courteous, and constitutionally unable to stop explaining. Six years of handling objects older than the building they sit in, and a habit of narrating what the hands are doing.

## Inventory
- [[The Glass Slate]] — his phone, a fifth of its charge left, **and forty-one photographs of a bronze mirror, both faces, raking light**
- A conservator's kit: cotton gloves, a soft brush, bamboo picks, a scalpel, a folding 10× loupe, a roll of acid-free tissue
- A notebook of rubbings and transcriptions, and two sticks of charcoal
- A steel vacuum flask of tea, still warm on Day 1
- Keys to a flat nine hundred years from here

## Knows
- He was alone in a basement store with a bronze disc on a foam block, photographing it for a condition report. The lights went, the rain outside went *upward* through the light-well, and the floor was water.
- Classical script, ritual bronze, and the vocabulary of pacts and offerings. He can read the sixty-four characters on the socket-stone at [[Reedmouth Marsh]] *badly but truly* — enough for "seat", "lent", "returns", "nine" — on Day 1, with no help from anyone.
- Court manners, forms of address, how to write a petition, and what a magistrate's clerk expects. He can pass for an educated man of a distant county, which is the only social armour he has.
- Modern things worth money or lives here: boiling water, washing hands, a splint, a clean dressing, arithmetic, double-entry books, why the kiln roof will fall.
- Nothing about cultivation, realms, sects or spirit roots. He knows, academically, that people believed in immortals. He is about to meet one.

## What his knowledge is worth here
He can start the real investigation on Day 1 — the stone is not a curiosity to him, it is an artefact with an inscription — and he is the one person in the prefecture who will eventually be able to read the ritual leaf [[Yan Buhui]] has carried in his robe for forty-one years. He is also physically the weakest adult in this story, and will be hurt by things a reed-cutter would shrug off.

## Secrets (GM only)
- He is tethered to [[The Mirror of Returning]]: root-stones stay grey, divination and [[Whisper-Listening]] slide off him, [[Ash Hounds]] cannot track him, the first [[Soul-Stilling Palm]] that lands on him fails — and he climbs one Insight cheaper per realm from Realm 2 up. He has no way to learn any of this except by living it.
- **The disc he was photographing is the Mirror**, nine hundred years older and in a museum's hands. He does not realise it until he compares his photographs to the frame fragment in [[The Sunken Kiln]] — and then he knows something nobody else in the world knows: that the Mirror survives, that it ends up in a glass case, and therefore that it leaves [[Cui Wangchuan]]'s courtyard somehow. Give him this only when he does the comparison, and never confirm *how*.
- His forty-one photographs are the most dangerous object in the prefecture. [[Luo Pingyan]] would recognise the disc instantly.
