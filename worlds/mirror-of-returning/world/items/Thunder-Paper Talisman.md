# Thunder-Paper Talisman (雷符纸)

- **Type:** Item (talisman — 符; single use)
- **Status:** One in existence locally, in the roof-thatch of [[Ruan Shiqi]]'s shack, taken off a body in [[Blackwater Gorge]] in Tianyou 19
- **Value:** 30 taels, or three spirit stones, or a favour nobody in Luyang can afford

A folded square of yellow reed paper, written in stone-dust ink by a hand that knew what it was doing. Slightly warm. It hums if held near a cultivator.

## What it does
Torn, it discharges once: a crack of white light and a sound like a tree splitting. **Treat it as a Realm 4 attack** — one target or a tight group, harm 3, and it does not care about realm gaps, armour or body-hardening. It is the reason a Realm 1 with a plan is not entirely helpless in front of a Golden Core.

Then it is ash, and it is the only one.

## Using it well
- It is a door-opener, an ambush, or a last word: at [[Blackwater Gorge]] from the rope bridge, in the kiln's upper chamber to bring the roof down, or in a courtyard where the alternative is the tithe cart leaving.
- Everything it hits and everyone who hears it knows a talisman was spent. Nobody in this prefecture will believe a reed-village stranger owned one honestly, and the questions afterwards are the real cost.
- Rain does not stop it. Being wet does not stop it. Having it in a pocket during a [[Soul-Stilling Palm]] does not help.

## Secrets (GM only)
- Ruan Shiqi will not use it and will not sell it. He will give it away, once, to someone who has done something he cannot do himself — and if it is given, the host should let it matter completely.
- The hand that wrote it was a Hollow Night talisman-master's, and [[Luo Pingyan]] would recognise the stroke order. Using it in front of her answers a question she has been asking about a dead courier since Tianyou 19.
