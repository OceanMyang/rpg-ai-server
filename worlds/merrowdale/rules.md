# Merrowdale: world rules

These add to the shared rules of play.

## Tone

Sun-scorched pastoral fantasy with a folk-horror chill: dust, cracked mud, cold deep water, and small people facing big choices. Keep the supernatural rare, quiet, and certain of itself — the valley is ordinary until it isn't.

## Checks — 2d6 + modifier

Roll 2d6 with modifier = the player character's stat + 1 if their Knack applies ± 1 for circumstances (tools, weather, preparation, injury) − the opposition's relevant ability rating (0–3, from its file). Clamp the modifier to −3..+3. Name the parts of the modifier when showing the roll: `🎲 Heart +1, the guard opposes (−2): 2d6−1 = 8 → mixed success`.

- **10+ Success** — they get what they wanted.
- **7–9 Mixed** — they get it, but with a cost, a complication, a hard choice, or only partly.
- **6 or less Failure** — it doesn't work *and* things get worse: harm, a lost item, an enemy acts, an alarm is raised, a clock advances, a secret comes out at a bad moment.

Stats: **Might** (force, endurance, fighting) · **Grace** (agility, stealth, precision) · **Wits** (noticing, knowing, deducing, deceiving) · **Heart** (persuading, empathy, courage, willpower).

## Tables — 1d100

Some entity files hold weighted tables — weather, encounters, rumors, bounties. The first column is a weight. Roll 1d100 and map it proportionally across the total weight to pick the row.

## The oracle

For a yes or no question about the world that no file answers — *is anyone home? does the smith have a crossbow?* — choose the odds from the files, roll 1d100, and abide by it.

| Odds | Yes on |
|---|---|
| Certain | 01–90 |
| Likely | 01–75 |
| Even | 01–50 |
| Unlikely | 01–25 |
| Remote | 01–10 |

A roll within 5 of the line is a *yes, but* or a *no, and* — a catch or an unexpected extra, never a flat reversal. Never ask the oracle something a file already answers.

## Harm

The player character has 6 Health. Harm is 1 (a punch, a scrape), 2 (a blade, a bite, a bad fall), or 3+ (deadly). At 0 they are dying or taken — resolve it from the fiction. A night's rest in safety restores 1 Health; [[Granny Nettle]]'s remedies restore more. Ordinary people and animals go down after 1–2 harm; anything with a rating of 2+ after 3–4.

## Time

Short conversation 10–30 min · searching a room 30 min · a meal 1 hour · travel per the Places & Travel table in `world/Merrowdale.md` · sleep 8 hours. When the day changes, advance the date in `game/state.md`, roll the day's weather, and check the clocks. A new game rolls the day's weather before the opening scene.

## Weather

Roll once per in-game day on the **Weather Table** in `world/Merrowdale.md`, then apply that file's **Active override** section before recording the result in `game/state.md`. The drought is an active setting: while [[The Rain Bell]] goes unrung, every rain result becomes dry — gathering cloud, dry thunder, thin sour mist — and not a drop falls anywhere in the valley. When the pact is renewed, drop the override and let rain fall the next day.

## Travel and encounters

Distances between places are in the Places & Travel table in `world/Merrowdale.md`. When the player travels between places, roll `1d6`; on a 1, roll on that file's **Encounter Table**, rerolling a night-only result by day.

## Rumors

When the player fishes for gossip at the inn, roll on the **Rumors Table** in `world/The Thirsty Heron.md`. Rumors are marked true, false, or half-true for you alone; characters repeat them as fact.

## Money

Silver pennies and copper bits, 10 copper to a silver. A laborer earns about a silver a week, so 20 silver is a life-changing reward to a villager here.
