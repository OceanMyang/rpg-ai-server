# Osmund Rook

- **Type:** Character (player character — human man, 45; he/him)
- **Status:** Alive
- **Location:** On the hill road, about to walk down into [[Dunmere]]
- **Concept:** A priest of the Dawn, defrocked for refusing to burn a village healer, now wandering and unsure what he still believes
- **Stats:** Heart +2, Wits +1, Might 0, Grace −1
- **Knack:** Church scripture and rites (+1 when it applies: quoting doctrine, arguing theology, performing or recognising rites)
- **Health:** 6/6
- **Conditions:** none
- **Silver:** 10 silver, 0 copper

## Description
Tall, heavy-set, and grey-bearded, with a deep, tired voice. His white-and-gold robe has had its sunburst unpicked, leaving a pale ghost of the shape on his chest. He still rises at dawn out of habit.

## Inventory
- A worn prayer book of the Dawn, heavily annotated in the margins
- A plain wooden walking staff
- A waterskin, about half full
- A patched travelling cloak and a bedroll

## Knows
- The Church of the Dawn calls the worship of local spirits demon-worship, and its witch-finders need a lord's writ to act on his lands.
- At Kettle Cross he heard that the Church has sent a witch-finder into Merrowdale, where a drought has lasted since Midsummer.
- The reeve of Dunmere has posted a notice offering **20 silver** for the return of the village's missing Rain Bell.
