# Gu Shuye (顾述野)

- **Type:** Character (human man, 118)
- **Status:** Alive
- **Realm:** 4 — Golden Core (金丹境)
- **Location:** The Bamboo Court, [[Mount Yinque]]
- **Role:** Head of [[The Green Bamboo Sect]] for 31 years
- **Stats:** Might +1, Grace +1, Wits +2, Heart +2
- **Abilities:** Sword 3, Sect law 2, Judging people 3
- **Qi:** 8/8 · **Health:** 10/10
- **Spirit root:** Earth (土)
- **Techniques:** [[Green Bamboo Spine]], [[Bamboo-Shadow Step]], the sect's top-shelf sword form

## Description
Looks fifty and is a hundred and eighteen. Grey at the temples, weathered hands, green robes mended at the cuff by his own hand. Sits very still. Does his own laundry in front of the disciples on purpose.

## Persona
- Quiet, dry, and slow to speak; asks two questions and then decides.
- Fair in a way that is not kind: he will tell a candidate the exact truth about their prospects.
- Unimpressed by talent and extremely impressed by people who keep their word at a cost.
- Carries the sect's survival like a stone. Every decision is measured against *does this outlive me*.
- **Tell:** when he has already decided and is letting you finish, he looks at the bamboo.

## Wants
- Nineteen disciples to become forty before he dies.
- To not hand his successor a war with [[The Hollow Night Sect]].
- To be wrong about what the tithe is.

## Knowledge
- Precisely what the sect can and cannot survive: four Golden Cores and a Void Gazing elder against his one core and nineteen children. He has done the arithmetic eight times.
- What the tithe is, roughly and without proof. He has refused to send a sect witness three years running.
- Realm, rank, merit, trials, and every technique on the mountain.
- That the sect's spirit vein is thinner at the top than it should be for its size, and that he does not want to know where the rest of it runs.

## Secrets (GM only)
- He will take a rootless Realm 0 as a **servant with a path** if they show him one real thing — nerve, a cleverness, a cleared nest, a debt owed by a disciple. He is not looking for talent; he is looking for someone who will still be here in ten years.
- He knows [[Jiang Sui'an]] is passing the register south, and has not stopped it, because the man's sister is dying and because a known leak is better than an unknown one. He is ashamed of this reasoning and uses it anyway.
- **Proof** changes him completely. Shown the Ledger Hall's records, or a witness the prefecture cannot dismiss, he will move — and he will move as a Golden Core who has spent 31 years being careful, which is the single most dangerous ally available in this story.
- He suspects [[Yan Buhui]] is a broken Foundation cultivator from the south and has deliberately never asked, out of a courtesy he would call cowardice.

## Relationships
- [[Xue Lingzhao]] — his only Foundation disciple and probable successor, and he does not like him, which he considers irrelevant.
- [[Bai Tinglu]] — three years overdue and he knows it; he has been waiting to see what she does about it.
- [[Jiang Sui'an]] — his steward, and the leak he tolerates.
- [[Luo Pingyan]] — they have met twice, politely. Both understood everything.

## History
- 104 years ago — a reed-village boy lights the root-stone ochre at fourteen and is taken at the trials. His family was paid in salt.
- 32 years ago — Golden Core.
- 31 years ago to now — head of the sect; 31 disciples down to 19.
- Tianyou 20, 21, 22 — refuses, three times, to send a witness against the tithe.
