# Ruan Shiqi (阮十七)

- **Type:** Character (human man, 22)
- **Status:** Alive
- **Realm:** 1 — Qi Sensing (引气境)
- **Location:** His shack north of the bridge, [[Luyang Town]]
- **Role:** Hunter, trapper, failed sect candidate; posts the bounties the sect will not take
- **Stats:** Might +1, Grace +2, Wits +1, Heart −1
- **Abilities:** Tracking 2, Snares and traps 2, Knife 1
- **Qi:** 2/2 · **Health:** 7/7
- **Techniques:** [[Reed-Breath Meditation]] (self-taught from a copied page, badly)

## Description
Rope-thin, weathered past his age, a shiny burn scar from jaw to collarbone. Deerhide coat, four knives, moves without noticing he is moving quietly.

## Persona
- Blunt to the point of rudeness; wastes no words and resents being made to repeat himself.
- Judges people by whether they are useful in the field and revises fast.
- Bitter about the sect in a way he denies: he failed the trials at sixteen, grey stone, and got to Realm 1 anyway by himself, which nobody has ever congratulated him for.
- Will not enter [[The Sunken Kiln]]. He has been to the mouth and that was enough, and he is honest about being afraid.

## Wants
- Enough silver to stop trapping before his hands go.
- To be told, once, by someone from the mountain, that a rootless man can climb.

## Knowledge
- The hills, the kiln, the gorge, the marsh, and every beast in them. Moth behaviour: they clump at dawn, they go for warmth and eyes, fire works and the kiln roof will not allow it.
- Bounties: what the sect pays and what it refuses. He funds the small ones himself out of hide money.
- That a rootless person can reach Realm 1 alone, because he did, on a stolen page, in four years, coughing blood twice.
- That a woman with a red umbrella was asking at the prefecture seat about strangers.

## Secrets (GM only)
- The page he learned from he took off a dead cultivator in the gorge in Tianyou 19, along with a talisman he has never dared use — a [[Thunder-Paper Talisman]], one shot, and it would flatten a Realm 3.
- His scar is a Hollow Night hound. He has never told anyone what he was doing on the south road that night: he was following the tithe cart, with a knife, at eighteen, and he turned back.
- He will teach [[Reed-Breath Meditation]] to anyone who asks him twice and does a full day's work first — which makes him the player character's fastest route to Realm 1 without the sect.

## Relationships
- [[Bai Tinglu]] — sect, and she treats him as an equal, which confuses him.
- [[Tao Xiaomai]] — follows him around; he pretends to mind.
- [[Yan Buhui]] — "a drunk who knows things." He has tried to get the old man to teach him and been laughed at.

## History
- Tianyou 17 — fails the Green Bamboo trials; the stone stays grey.
- Tianyou 19 — takes a page and a talisman off a body in [[Blackwater Gorge]]; follows the tithe cart and turns back; gets the scar.
- Tianyou 21 — reaches Realm 1, alone, and tells nobody for a month.
