# Reedmouth Marsh (芦口泽)

- **Type:** Place (marsh; the arrival site)
- **Where:** 15 minutes south of [[Luyang Town]] along a raised mud path
- **Status:** Flooded to the knee; the socket-stone is uncovered for the first time in years

## Overview
Where the channel loses its banks: eight-foot reeds, black water, snipe, and paths that are only paths in dry months. Eel traps on poles. Reed-cutters work the eastern edge in the morning and are gone by noon, because the marsh is where the town's drowned turn up.

## The socket-stone (承镜石)
Twenty feet off the path, in a stand of dead reeds, a slab of grey granite stands hip-high and slightly out of true. Cut into its face is a circular socket the size of a dinner plate, empty, its rim polished. Around the rim, worn nearly flat, sixty-four small characters in a hand nobody in the prefecture reads.

The stone is warm. In cold rain it steams. Children are told not to touch it and do anyway, and nothing has ever happened to them.

It is the eighth of the nine anchor-stones of [[The Mirror of Returning]], and on Day 1, for about two heartbeats, it was the loudest object in three prefectures.

## Things a careful person finds here
- The player character's own drag-mark, ending nowhere — no footprints leading to where they woke.
- Sixty-four characters that a scholar could copy in charcoal and puzzle over later. They are old Yun ritual script, and they say, in part: *"…the eighth seat. What is lent returns. Nine seats, one road, and the road runs both ways."*
- An eel-trap with a good knife lost in the mud beside it, if somebody thinks to search.
- Frog-spawn, and no birds at all within twenty feet of the stone.

## Secrets (GM only)
- Touching the stone with bare hands, at any realm, gives a single flash: rain falling **upward**, and a man's voice, very far off, saying *"—hold it, hold it, you'll lose the—"*. Once per player character. It grants +1 Insight the first time and nothing after.
- The stone will wake again when the Mirror is used, wherever the Mirror is. If the player character is standing on it at that moment, the road opens from this end.
- [[Yan Buhui]] knows what a socket-stone is and has been drinking two hundred yards from one for nine years without knowing it was here. When he learns, he sobers up for a day.
