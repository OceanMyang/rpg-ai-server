# Soul-Stilling Palm (定魂掌)

- **Type:** Technique (soul art — 魂术)
- **Element:** none — it works on the soul, not the body
- **Requires:** Realm 3
- **Cost:** 2 Qi · **Bonus:** +1; ignores armour, body-hardening and pain entirely
- **Held by:** [[Luo Pingyan]], every Hollow Night disciple of Realm 3 or better, [[Cui Wangchuan]] — and [[Yan Buhui]], who will not use it
- **Where:** nowhere the player can lawfully learn it

An open palm laid against the chest, the neck, or the back of the head. Nothing breaks. The target's soul goes still, and their body goes down with it, and they wake up hours later with a nosebleed and an unshakeable conviction that something is missing.

## Effect
- A hit stills a target of equal or lower realm: unconscious, unharmed, unable to resist being carried. **This is why capture, not death, is the Hollow Night's default** — and why losing to them continues the story.
- Against a target two or more realms below, the user chooses: still them, or press, and pressing means the soul does not come all the way back. Repeated pressing is how a vessel is prepared.
- [[Green Bamboo Spine]] does nothing against it. Body-hardening is the wrong wall.

## The only defences
Distance. Not being touched. Numbers. A talisman like the [[Thunder-Paper Talisman]] going off first. And the player character's tether — a Mirror-tethered soul is *slippery*: the first Soul-Stilling Palm that lands on them fails, visibly, and frightens whoever threw it.

## Secrets (GM only)
- That failure is the moment the antagonists become certain of what the player character is. Play it as a victory that costs them their anonymity.
- Yan Buhui knows this technique better than anyone north of the Terrace, and could teach the counter-breathing that halves it — one night of teaching, and he will hate every minute of it.
