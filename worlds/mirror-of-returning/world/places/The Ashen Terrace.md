# The Ashen Terrace (烬台)

- **Type:** Place (mountain seat of [[The Hollow Night Sect]])
- **Where:** far south, past three prefectures — 20 days on foot from [[Luyang Town]]
- **Status:** Out of reach, and should stay that way for a long time

## Overview
A basalt shelf on a dead volcano above a valley where nothing has grown for a century. Nine black terraces step up the slope; on the ninth stands the Ash Hall, and behind it the **Cinder Well (烬井)**, where the sect burns what it has bought. The air tastes of struck flint. The sect keeps four hundred disciples, four Golden Cores, and a Void Gazing elder who does not come out.

## What is known in Cangping
Almost nothing. That the tithe cart goes south. That the sect's people wear black with an ash-grey collar and are polite. That a magistrate two prefectures over once sent men and got his own seal back in a box, alone.

## The shape of the place, for a much later day
- Nine terraces, each gated, each with its own patrol; the first three are disciples' quarters and a market and can be walked by anyone in sect black.
- The **Cinder Well** on the ninth terrace, and beside it the elder's own courtyard, where [[Cui Wangchuan]] keeps [[The Mirror of Returning]] on a stand behind a curtain of ash-silk. He wears it at his belt only when he travels.
- The **Ledger Hall** on the sixth: every tally, every name, every child, in order. This building is worth more than the Mirror to anyone who wants the sect destroyed rather than robbed.
- The sect's own law, the **Nine Articles (九律)**, carved at the first gate. Elders are bound by them too, and Cui Wangchuan has broken the fourth for two centuries.

## Running it
Do not let the player reach this place early, and do not soften it when they do. The distance is 20 days; the gates are real; every person inside is Realm 2 or better. The way in is not stealth at Realm 3 — it is sect black, an invitation, a tithe cart, a rival elder's favour, or being carried through the gate as a prize, which is exactly what [[Luo Pingyan]] is for.

## Secrets (GM only)
- Cui Wangchuan cannot leave the Terrace for more than nine days: the Cinder Well is what keeps his cracked core from failing. Everything he wants, he must have brought to him. This is why the world's most dangerous man is not simply walking north.
- Three of the four Golden Cores resent the tithe going to one elder's core. The sect can be turned against him with the Ledger Hall's own records, by someone with no realm at all.
- The fourth of the Nine Articles: *no elder shall spend the sect's substance on his own body.* Proving it is a death sentence for him under his own law — and the trial would be held by people just as bad.
