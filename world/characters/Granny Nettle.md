# Granny Nettle

- **Type:** Character (human woman, 67)
- **Status:** Alive
- **Location:** Her hut in [[Thornwood]]
- **Real name:** Hester Wynn — few remember it
- **Role:** Hedge witch: herbalist, midwife, charm-maker. The villagers come to her in secret and curse her in public.
- **Abilities:** Herbcraft and remedies 3, Minor charms 2, Reading people 2, Old Wendle script 1

## Description
Small and bent, with a hawk's nose, bright black eyes, and hands brown as roots. A patchwork shawl hung with little bone and glass charms that click when she moves. A one-eyed crow called Sexton rides her shoulder. Smells of woodsmoke and thyme.

## Persona
- Sharp-tongued, sardonic, impossible to flatter. Respects bluntness and courage; despises whining.
- Nothing is free: a remedy or an answer costs a favor, a story, or something odd — "a pike's tooth", "a secret you've never told anyone".
- Under the vinegar: old grief, and fierce kindness toward [[Wren]].
- Bitter toward the Lady, whom she calls "the Old Hag in the Water".

## Wants
- To see Tam Ashby again.
- To keep Wren from going the way Tam went.
- To be left alone — though she won't run from [[Brother Caddock]]: "I was here before his god came to this valley."

## Knowledge
- The old tales of the pact. Her grandmother told her "the first words are written in the chapel of Wendle, under the water."
- She knows this is a fiftieth year, and has guessed that Maren hid the bell to protect Wren. She has told no one.
- Remedies: she can break a fever, draw poison, brew a sleeping draught, and knot a **drowning-charm** — a cord that keeps the wearer from being drawn under by the Lady for one night. (Takes an hour to make.)
- She knows Caddock means to burn her.

## Secrets (GM only)
- Fifty years ago she loved **[[Tam Ashby]]**. On clear full-moon nights she still hears him singing out on the lake. She believes he lives on, somehow, and would give anything to see him.
- She will help anyone who promises — and means it — to help her reach Tam.

## Relationships
- [[Tam Ashby]] — her lost love.
- [[Wren]] — fond of her; sees Tam's fate coming for her.
- [[Sister Maren]] — respects her; they argue about the Lady.
- [[Brother Caddock]] — her would-be executioner.

## History
- 1097, Midsummer's Eve — Tam rings the bell and walks into the lake. That autumn Hester leaves the village for the woods and becomes "Granny Nettle".
- 3 days before Day 1 — Caddock preaches that "the witch in the wood" brought the drought.
