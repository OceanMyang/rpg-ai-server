# Dunmere

- **Type:** Location (village)
- **Status:** Suffering — water rationed, crops failing, tempers short
- **Region:** [[Merrowdale]], on the east shore of [[Merrow Lake]]
- **Population:** about 200 — farmers, fishers, a smith, a miller whose wheel no longer turns
- **Headman:** [[Corwin Hale]], the reeve

## Description
Whitewashed, thatched cottages climbing a gentle slope above the lake. The thatch is dry as tinder and the gardens are dust. The green, once lush, is yellow straw around the old stone well, where a queue forms every dawn. The jetty now ends in cracked mud thirty yards from the water; the fishing boats lie on their sides like dead animals.

## Notable Places
- **The green and the well** — the heart of the village. Corwin hands out water rations here at dawn. [[Brother Caddock]] preaches here at dawn and dusk.
- **[[The Thirsty Heron]]** — the inn, on the green.
- **The reeve's house** — Corwin's, beside the green.
- **The smithy** — cold. Garrow the smith, a huge man, has become Caddock's loudest follower.
- **The mill** — at the lake's southern outflow below the Vance Weir; its wheel is dry.
- **The lake path** — north along the shore to the [[Mere Shrine]] (10 minutes).
- **The hill road** — east, out of the valley.

## Mood
- One bucket of water per household per day, and the well is nearly dry (see Clocks in `game/state.md`).
- Several families have already left over the hill road.
- The village is splitting: some still pray at the Mere Shrine; more gather each evening to hear Brother Caddock.
- Strangers are watched — a stranger might be a water thief or "the witch's creature". The reward notice has brought a few fortune-seekers already; none found anything.
