# Cangping Prefecture (苍坪郡)

- **Type:** Region (river prefecture of [[The Great Yun Dynasty]])
- **Status:** Ninth month, cold rain. Harvest tax collected; the tithe cart is due.
- **Seat:** Cangping town, two days east — a magistrate, a garrison of forty, a pawnshop that deals in spirit stones
- **Sect of record:** [[The Green Bamboo Sect]] on [[Mount Yinque]]

## Overview
Flat reed country cut by the Yun-Hua channel, rising west into low hills of grey clay where the kilns are. Indigo, coarse porcelain, reed paper, eels. It floods every third spring and nobody has ever been rich here. Cultivators pass through and rarely stop: there is one minor sect, no spirit vein worth the name, and nothing to want.

That is exactly why the eighth socket-stone was hidden here. See [[The Mirror of Returning]].

## History
- Tianyou 20 — the soul-tithe reaches Cangping for the first time; four children leave Luyang on the cart.
- Tianyou 21 — a bad flood; the magistrate's granary is found empty and he is not punished.
- Tianyou 23, eighth month — the harvest tax is collected in full for the first time in six years.
