# Chronicle

Append-only record of significant events, oldest first. One line each: `Day N, time — what happened`.

## Before play
- ~400 years ago — The founders of Wendle make a pact with [[The Lady of the Mere]]: rain for the valley, in return for remembrance and the ringing of [[The Rain Bell]].
- ~160 years ago — The Vance family builds the weir; [[Merrow Lake]] rises and drowns [[Old Wendle]]. The survivors found [[Dunmere]].
- 50 years ago (1097) — [[Tam Ashby]] rings the bell on Midsummer's Eve and walks into the lake. Never seen again.
- Midsummer's Eve, 61 days before Day 1 — The Rain Bell is found missing from the [[Mere Shrine]] (it vanished the night before); Bell Night cannot be held. No rain has fallen in [[Merrowdale]] since.
- 10 days before Day 1 — [[Corwin Hale]] writes to the Church for help and posts a 20-silver reward notice at the crossroads town.
- 3 days before Day 1 — [[Brother Caddock]] arrives with a writ from [[Lord Vance]] and starts preaching against "the witch in the wood".
- 2 days before Day 1 — [[Fennick]] the peddler returns with barrels of water to sell.
