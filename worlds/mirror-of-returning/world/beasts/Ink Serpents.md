# Ink Serpents (墨蛇)

- **Type:** Beast (Realm 2, solitary)
- **Status:** Breeding in the slack pools at the north end of [[Blackwater Gorge]]
- **Rating:** 2 · **Health:** 4 · **Harm:** 2, and the bite does not stop bleeding

Eight feet of black water-snake, dry-skinned, and the water goes cloudy where it swims. It hunts by the shiver a walking man puts into mud.

## Behaviour
- Strikes from water at anything on the bank within four feet, once, then withdraws to see what is left.
- Its bite thins blood: 1 harm a day after until bound properly. A modern person who knows pressure, elevation and a clean tight dressing removes this entirely, and every local remedy — mud, ash, prayer, a hot iron — makes it worse.
- Afraid of fire on water, and of noise it cannot place.
- The core in its skull is worth one spirit stone, and the skin is worth two taels to a bootmaker in the seat.

## Why they are on the board
The 8-merit bounty two disciples turned down. A Realm 1 with a spear, a partner, and the sense to fight it out of the water can take one; a Realm 1 who wades in cannot. It is the honest measure of whether the player character has understood the difference between realm and preparation.
