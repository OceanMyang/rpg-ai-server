# The Mirror of Returning (归溯镜)

- **Type:** Item (treasure — 至宝; nine hundred years old, maker disputed)
- **Status:** In the courtyard of the ninth terrace, [[The Ashen Terrace]], behind a curtain of ash-silk
- **Held by:** [[Cui Wangchuan]] for 212 years
- **The player character's only road home**

A bronze disc the width of two hands, black with age, its back cast with reeds and water and sixty-four characters of old Yun ritual script. The face is not polished bronze: it is dull, and things reflected in it arrive a half-beat late.

## What it does
It lends and takes back. Set in one of its nine **socket-stones (承镜石)**, worked by someone who knows the ritual, it moves a living thing out of its own year. The characters on the rim say, in part: *"…the eighth seat. What is lent returns. Nine seats, one road, and the road runs both ways."*

Nine socket-stones were set across the southern prefectures when it was made. Eight are known to Cui Wangchuan. The eighth stands in [[Reedmouth Marsh]], a quarter hour from [[Luyang Town]], and he knows only the prefecture.

## Why it is not simply stolen
It hangs in the courtyard of a Realm 6 elder, on a mountain of four hundred, twenty days south. See `rules.md`: a gap of four or more realms is not a roll. There is no plan at Realm 0 that ends with this object in the player character's hands.

## What is findable, and roughly when
1. **The socket-stone** — Day 1, immediately, and meaningless without someone who can read it.
2. **The frame fragment** in the kiln-god niche at [[The Sunken Kiln]] — a cracked-off arc of a bronze frame whose characters match the socket-stone's rim. Proof the thing is real and has parts.
3. **[[Yan Buhui]]**, who can read the script, name the Mirror, and say whose courtyard it hangs in — rationed out over many turns, and gone on Day 12 if he dies.
4. **The Ledger Hall** at the Ashen Terrace, which records two hundred and eleven failed attempts to use it, in order, with names.

## Secrets (GM only)
- **It answers only the one it pulled.** Cui Wangchuan has tried it on two hundred and eleven people in two centuries. The player character can work it and he cannot, and that is the whole balance of power in this story: he needs them alive, whole, and willing.
- **The toll.** A passage of nine hundred years requires a life anchored in the destination year, given willingly. The leaf in the lining of Yan Buhui's robe says so. Cui Wangchuan intends to pay it with a soul held in the Cinder Well — [[Luo Pingyan]]'s daughter.
- The player character's tether runs to this object. It is why root-stones stay grey, why divination slides off them, why they climb one Insight cheaper per realm, and why a [[Soul-Stilling Palm]] fails on them the first time.
- If it is ever worked from the marsh end, the socket-stone wakes wherever the Mirror is, and the passage opens from [[Reedmouth Marsh]] — which means the endgame does not have to happen on the ninth terrace.
- **Do not let the player touch it, see it, or learn item 4 before the ladder has been climbed.** The Mirror is the horizon, not a puzzle. There is no scheduled ending: they may go home, or stay, or never manage it, and none of those is a loss.
