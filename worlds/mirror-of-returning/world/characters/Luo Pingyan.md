# Luo Pingyan (罗屏烟)

- **Type:** Character (human woman, 91 — looks 35)
- **Status:** Alive
- **Realm:** 4 — Golden Core (金丹境)
- **Location:** The prefecture seat (Days 1–5); [[Luyang Town]] from Day 6; with the tithe cart on Day 8
- **Role:** Tithe collector of [[The Hollow Night Sect]] in [[Cangping Prefecture]]; the mid-game antagonist
- **Stats:** Might +1, Grace +2, Wits +2, Heart +1
- **Abilities:** Sword 2, Reading people 3, Divination 2, Sect law 2
- **Qi:** 8/8 · **Health:** 10/10
- **Spirit root:** Water (水), pure
- **Techniques:** [[Soul-Stilling Palm]], [[Whisper-Listening]], [[Bamboo-Shadow Step]] (a version of it)

## Description
Plain dark travelling clothes, no ornament, a red oiled-paper umbrella she keeps open in rain and shut and dry in her hand indoors. A ledger in a lacquered case. She looks thirty-five and moves like water finding a level. Voice pitched for one listener at a time.

## Persona
- Kind, and it is not an act, which is the horror of her. She remembers names, children's names, what a family is short of.
- Never threatens. States what will happen, once, in a level voice, and it is always exactly what happens.
- Keeps every promise. This is her whole method: the prefecture cannot point to a single lie.
- Hates waste, including cruelty, and will stop a hound-handler who enjoys the work.
- **Tell:** she closes the umbrella before she says the true thing.

## Wants
- This year's tithe, complete and papered.
- The rootless stranger, alive, unharmed, and willing — she would far rather be given them than take them.
- One thing she is owed by [[Cui Wangchuan]], which she has been earning for thirty years and will not name.

## Knowledge
- Every root-stone result in the prefecture for six years, and which house each child sleeps in. She has this from [[Jiang Sui'an]].
- A divination on the night of Day 1 that something *lent* was returned in the reed country south of Luyang. She does not know what came through, only where and roughly when.
- That a person who reads as a hole is not rootless but tethered; she has seen it once, eleven years ago.
- Exactly what [[The Green Bamboo Sect]] can field, and that it is nineteen children and a tired Golden Core.
- The Nine Articles, word for word, and that her master breaks the fourth.

## Secrets (GM only)
- **She is Article 4's proof.** She keeps a second ledger in a hand nobody else reads: two hundred and six names of children she sent to the Cinder Well, and the tally of how many went to one elder's core. She has never used it. She is the key to destroying him and she is the last person who will volunteer.
- The thing she is owed: her own daughter's name is in that ledger, entered by her own hand in Tianyou 3 under duress, and she has been promised the girl's soul is *held*, not spent. She has never been allowed to see her. She may be lying to herself.
- She will not kill the player character and will not let them be badly hurt. Her orders, once Cui Wangchuan knows, are: alive, whole, willing if possible. Play this openly — at Realm 0 to 3, losing to her means captivity, a long road south, and the story continuing, not death.
- Her offer, made calmly and truthfully around Day 6: come south, be fed, be taught properly by people who can actually teach, and be shown the Mirror. Every word of it is true. She simply will not say what happens on the ninth day.

## Relationships
- [[Cui Wangchuan]] — her master, her terror, her creditor.
- [[Jiang Sui'an]] — her asset; she is genuinely gentle with him and sends the pills on time.
- [[Gu Shuye]] — met twice, politely. She would rather not; he is old, careful, and would cost her the prefecture if he ever moved.
- [[Yan Buhui]] — a name in an old register, fourth disciple, absconded. She does not know he is two hundred yards from the socket-stone.

## History
- 91 years ago — born in a reed village not unlike Luyang.
- 78 years ago — lights the root-stone blue at thirteen; taken south.
- Tianyou 3 — her daughter is entered in the tithe ledger. She writes the name herself.
- Tianyou 12 — the boy who read as a hole is taken south; she was told he was a gift to the elder, and she was not told what for.
- Tianyou 17 — recruits [[Jiang Sui'an]] with four pills a year.
- Tianyou 23, night of Day 1 — her divination burns through three sheets of paper and she stops sleeping.
