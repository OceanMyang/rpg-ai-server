# Marrow-Warming Pill (暖髓丹)

- **Type:** Item (pill — 丹药; consumable)
- **Where:** [[The Green Bamboo Sect]]'s pill store — 8 merit, two in stock; the prefecture seat's apothecary at 6 taels and probably adulterated
- **Also:** four a year go to [[Jiang Sui'an]]'s sister, sent by [[Luo Pingyan]]

A waxy brown ball the size of a cherry stone, tasting of liquorice and hot iron. The cheapest real pill in the south, and the one that turns a stalled cultivator into a climbing one.

## Effects — choose one, when taken
- **+1 to a breakthrough attempt**, if taken at the start of the meditation.
- **+1 Insight**, once per realm — the first one is a revelation and the fourth is a sweet.
- **Heal 2 Health** and clear one condition of cold, exhaustion or a bad wound going sour.
- Given to a dying cultivator with a cracked core, it buys **three months**. This is the only thing in the prefecture that will keep [[Yan Buhui]] past Day 12.

## Limits
One per day, and a second within a day is 1 harm and no benefit. Mortals get warmth, a sound night's sleep, and nothing else — it is not medicine, and it will not save [[Jiang Sui'an]]'s sister, only postpone her.

## Why it is on the board
It is the clearest object lesson in how the ladder is actually climbed: **merit buys pills, pills buy realms.** Eight merit is two bounties. The player character's first real economic decision is whether that goes into their own breakthrough or into the old drunk who knows where the Mirror is.
