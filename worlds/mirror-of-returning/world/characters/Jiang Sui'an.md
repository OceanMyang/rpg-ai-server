# Jiang Sui'an (江随安)

- **Type:** Character (human man, 49)
- **Status:** Alive
- **Realm:** 2 — Qi Refining (炼气境)
- **Location:** The Scripture Loft and the ledger room, [[Mount Yinque]]
- **Role:** Steward of [[The Green Bamboo Sect]] — merit ledger, loft key, disciple register
- **Stats:** Might −1, Grace 0, Wits +2, Heart +1
- **Abilities:** Accounts 3, Sect records 3, Forging a hand 2
- **Qi:** 4/4 · **Health:** 8/8
- **Spirit root:** Wood (木), weak
- **Techniques:** [[Reed-Breath Meditation]]

## Description
Soft-spoken, stooped, ink on his sleeve, a ring of keys he never lets rattle. Thirty years on the mountain and never past Realm 2. Remembers every disciple's name, birth month and merit balance without looking.

## Persona
- Genuinely kind. Finds the loophole that lets a servant eat, the errand that pays a candidate's road home.
- Cannot say no to anyone in front of him, which is his entire character.
- Speaks well of everybody, including people he is betraying.
- **Tell:** when he lies he is *more* helpful, not less — he over-explains and offers you tea.

## Wants
- His younger sister in the prefecture seat to live through another winter.
- To not be found out, and, underneath that, to be found out by someone who will make him stop.

## Knowledge
- The ledger: every disciple's merit, every bounty ever posted, who owes whom.
- The loft's catalogue and which manuals are missing (four, since Tianyou 16, three of them his).
- Every root-stone result the sect has recorded in six years, village by village, child by child.
- Sect procedure well enough to bend it: a servant's place, an early trial slot, a manual signed out to a name that does not exist.

## Secrets (GM only)
- **He is the leak.** For six years he has sent [[Luo Pingyan]] the sect's register and — worse — the root-stone results from the villages the sect tests. The tithe cart knows which doors to knock on because of him. In exchange, four pills a year keep his sister alive.
- He does not think of himself as a traitor. He has a list of reasons, in the back of the ledger, in his own hand.
- He knows the Hollow Night sounded out [[Xue Lingzhao]], because he carried the message.
- [[Gu Shuye]] already knows and tolerates it. Jiang Sui'an does not know that he knows, and finding out breaks him.
- He will help the player character generously and sincerely, and report them south the same evening. This should be discovered, not guessed — through the ledger's back pages, a pill bottle with a Hollow Night seal, or the timing of who arrives after the PC asks a question on the mountain.

## Relationships
- [[Gu Shuye]] — his head, whom he loves and has sold.
- [[Bai Tinglu]] — his favourite; he has quietly credited her merit twice.
- [[Luo Pingyan]] — "the lady in the seat". He has met her four times and thrown up afterwards twice.

## History
- 30 years ago — arrives as a servant, promoted for his handwriting.
- Tianyou 17 — his sister's lungs begin to fail.
- Tianyou 17, ninth month — a woman with a red umbrella finds him at the prefecture market and is very kind.
