# Mount Yinque (隐雀山)

- **Type:** Place (mountain; seat of [[The Green Bamboo Sect]])
- **Where:** half a day north of [[Luyang Town]]
- **Status:** Preparing the outer-disciple trials for Day 5

## Overview
Not a great mountain — 1,400 feet of wet green rock with a bamboo forest on its skirts and cloud on it most mornings. A stone stair of 1,308 steps goes up from the Sparrow Gate to the sect's courtyards. Mortals may climb to the gate and no further without a token.

## Up the mountain
- **The Sparrow Gate (雀门)** — an arch of green stone at the bottom of the stair, two Realm 1 gate disciples, bored, wet, and empowered to turn anyone away. The bounty board hangs here, and on trial days the register table.
- **The Long Stair** — 1,308 steps. Servants carry rice up it daily. A Realm 0 climb takes two hours and hurts for a day; this is the sect's first and crudest test, and they watch who stops.
- **The Bamboo Court (竹庭)** — the sect's yard, drill-ground, and the hall where [[Gu Shuye]] holds audience. Nineteen disciples' names on a board, six of them blacked out.
- **The Servants' Row (杂役房)** — kitchens, wood store, hides for the dye trade, and eleven servants who are not disciples and never will be. The likeliest way in for a rootless stranger.
- **The Scripture Loft (经楼)** — three shelves of technique manuals, ranked by rank. Outer disciples reach the bottom shelf only. [[Jiang Sui'an]] keeps the key and the borrowing ledger.
- **The Four Caves (四窟)** — qi-rich hollows in the rock face where the sect's thin spirit vein surfaces. A night in one is worth +1 Insight, once per cave per realm. Cave time is bought with merit, 5 merit a night, and the inner disciples have first claim.
- **The Sparrow Terrace** — a flat shelf near the summit, bamboo to the edge, where a breakthrough attempt gets +1. Also where people go to be alone, which in a sect of nineteen means it is never free.

## Standing and access

| Rank | May go | May take |
|---|---|---|
| Servant (杂役) | Row, kitchens, stair | nothing; 8 coin a day and food |
| Outer disciple (外门弟子) | Bamboo Court, bottom shelf, Cave Four | bounties up to 5 merit |
| Inner disciple (内门弟子) | all four caves, second shelf | bounties up to 15 merit; a stipend of 2 stones a month |
| Core disciple (真传弟子) | top shelf, the Terrace on demand | anything |

## How the mountain feels to a modern person
Wet, poor, and strict. Nineteen disciples in patched green, one Golden Core who does his own laundry, rice twice a day, and a bell at four in the morning. There is no mystery about it and no comfort in it, and it is still the single best thing that could happen to them.

## History
- Founded 420 years ago by a Foundation cultivator who liked the bamboo.
- Tianyou 12 — thirty-one disciples. Tianyou 23 — nineteen.
- Tianyou 20 — [[Gu Shuye]] refuses to send a witness against the first tithe collection. Two disciples leave over it.
