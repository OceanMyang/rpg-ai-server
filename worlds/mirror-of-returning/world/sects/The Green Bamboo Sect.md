# The Green Bamboo Sect (青竹宗)

- **Type:** Sect (minor, third rate)
- **Seat:** [[Mount Yinque]], [[Cangping Prefecture]]
- **Head:** [[Gu Shuye]] — Realm 4, Golden Core
- **Strength:** 19 disciples (1 Golden Core, 1 Foundation, 9 Qi Refining, 8 Qi Sensing), 11 servants
- **Status:** Poor, shrinking, honest, and about to hold its outer-disciple trials on Day 5

The door the player character is meant to walk through. Not a temple of wonders: a struggling business with nineteen employees, a mountain, a thin spirit vein, and rice twice a day.

## What it is for
The sect sells protection: it clears beasts, settles hauntings, and keeps a token presence so that no worse sect claims [[Cangping Prefecture]]. The prefecture pays in grain, dye and reed paper. It is not enough.

## Ranks and how to rise

| Rank | Requirement | What it opens |
|---|---|---|
| **Servant (杂役)** | a pulse, and someone to vouch | food, a dry bed, 8 coin a day, and the run of the Servants' Row. No techniques. |
| **Outer disciple (外门弟子)** | pass the trials, **or** 20 merit as a servant and an elder's word | green robes, [[Reed-Breath Meditation]], the bottom shelf of the Scripture Loft, Cave Four, bounties to 5 merit |
| **Inner disciple (内门弟子)** | Realm 2 **and** 40 merit | all four caves, second shelf, 2 spirit stones a month, bounties to 15 merit, a say in the Bamboo Court |
| **Core disciple (真传弟子)** | Realm 3 **and** an elder taking you as their own | the top shelf, the Sparrow Terrace on demand, and the sect's ear |
| **Elder (长老)** | Realm 4 | a vote, and the sect's debts |

**Merit (功绩)** is the sect's ledger of what you are owed. Earned from bounties and sect errands, spent on cave nights (5), manuals (10–30), pills (8 for a [[Marrow-Warming Pill]]), spirit stones (10 merit each), and rank. [[Jiang Sui'an]] keeps the ledger.

## The trials (Day 5, dawn)
Open to anyone under twenty-five who climbs the stair. Three parts, run in the Bamboo Court in front of everyone:
1. **The stair** — 1,308 steps, and they time you. A Realm 0 who arrives on their feet and still breathing has already passed something.
2. **The root-stone** — both hands on the quartz. Lights = taken regardless of the rest. Grey = you are not refused, you are simply not wanted, and everything after this is uphill.
3. **The bout** — three exchanges against a Qi Sensing disciple with a bamboo staff. They are not trying to hurt you. Losing well beats winning luckily, and [[Gu Shuye]] watches the face, not the staff.

A rootless Realm 0 who shows something real — nerve, a cleverness nobody there has seen, a debt owed by a disciple, a moth nest cleared last week — can be taken as a **servant with a path**, which is the honest best case and exactly enough.

## Politics
- **Gu Shuye** wants the sect to survive him, and knows exactly what the tithe is. He has not acted, and it is eating him.
- **[[Xue Lingzhao]]** — Realm 3, the sect's only Foundation disciple, and its future. Proud, correct, and privately certain the sect is beneath him.
- **[[Jiang Sui'an]]** — steward, keeper of the ledger and the loft key. Kind, tired, and the reason the sect's secrets are not secret.
- **[[Bai Tinglu]]** — outer disciple, three years overdue for inner, and short-handed on a job she cannot fail.

## Techniques the sect holds
[[Reed-Breath Meditation]] (given at outer), [[Bamboo-Shadow Step]] (bottom shelf, 10 merit), [[Green Bamboo Spine]] (second shelf, 25 merit), and one top-shelf sword form that only Xue Lingzhao and Gu Shuye have read.

## Secrets (GM only)
- The sect's thin spirit vein is the tail of something larger that runs south-west under the clay hills — through the lower flue of [[The Sunken Kiln]] and on toward [[Reedmouth Marsh]]. Gu Shuye suspects. If a stronger sect ever learns, the Green Bamboo Sect ends.
- Jiang Sui'an has been passing the sect's disciple register and root-stone results to [[Luo Pingyan]] for six years, in exchange for a pill that keeps his sister alive. He is not evil and he will not stop.
