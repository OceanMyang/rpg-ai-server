# The Sunken Kiln (陷窑)

- **Type:** Place (ruin; the first dungeon)
- **Where:** 1½ hours west of [[Luyang Town]], in the grey clay hills
- **Status:** Infested — [[Corpse Moths]] hatching in the flue since 21 days before Day 1

## Overview
A dragon-kiln of the old type: a brick tunnel eighty feet long driven into a hillside, collapsed at the middle so the hill has swallowed its own roof. It fired coarse porcelain for two hundred years and was abandoned when the clay soured. Shards everywhere, ankle-deep, and they cut.

Three weeks ago kiln-diggers looking for firebrick broke through the collapse into the sealed lower flue. Two of them have not come back for their pay.

## Inside
1. **The mouth** — half-buried, propped with a beam somebody set badly. Bat stink. A digger's basket, a straw hat, one sandal.
2. **The upper chamber** — dry, shard-floored, and the roof is the collapse itself: loose brick and clay held up by nothing. Loud noise or a fight brings part of it down (a modern eye sees this at once, no roll).
3. **The break** — a gap into the lower flue at ankle height, with cold air coming out of it. Grey silk in the cracks, thick as felt. Eleven moth pupae on Day 1, forty by Day 4.
4. **The lower flue** — sealed since the kiln was built and warm, with a thread of the prefecture's spirit vein running along its floor. Qi-rich: a night's meditation here is +1 Insight (once per realm) if one can sleep among the moths.
5. **The kiln-god niche (窑神龛)** — at the far end, a soot-black clay figure with a bronze ring at its feet, and behind the figure a cavity holding a bundle in oiled cloth.

## The bundle
A kiln-master's hoard, hidden about nine hundred years ago when the anchor-stones were set: three low spirit stones, a brass hand-mirror the size of a palm with a **cracked-off arc of its rim** — and the arc does not belong to the brass mirror at all. It is a fragment of a frame, and the characters on it match the rim of the socket-stone at [[Reedmouth Marsh]].

This is the first hard clue that [[The Mirror of Returning]] exists and is a real object with a real owner.

## Running the place
- The moths are Realm 1 things with rating 1, harmless one at a time, murderous in a swarm; they hunt warmth and go for the eyes.
- Fire works and the roof does not permit it, which is the whole tactical puzzle.
- The bounty on the nest is 2 taels from [[Ruan Shiqi]], or 5 merit from the sect if [[Bai Tinglu]] takes the job with the PC's help.
- Clearing it is worth +2 Insight (a mission that cost something), and it is survivable at Realm 0 **with a plan and a partner** — flooding the flue, smoking them at dawn when they clump, or collapsing the break deliberately.

## Secrets (GM only)
- The moths are not native. The digger who broke through was carrying a Hollow Night tally as a good-luck charm and the eggs came in on it three years ago, dormant. [[Luo Pingyan]] knows what a moth nest means and will know someone has been in here.
- The two missing diggers are in the lower flue, cocooned, one of them still alive on Day 1 and not by Day 4.
