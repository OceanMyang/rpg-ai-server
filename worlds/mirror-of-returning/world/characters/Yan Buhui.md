# Yan Buhui (言不回)

- **Type:** Character (human man, 63)
- **Status:** Alive — dying of a forty-year-old qi deviation
- **Realm:** 3 — Foundation Building (筑基境), core cracked; fights like a desperate Realm 2
- **Location:** The corner mat by the door, The Broken Oar, [[Luyang Town]]
- **Role:** Drunk; former disciple of [[The Hollow Night Sect]]; the only man in the prefecture who knows what took the player character
- **Stats:** Might 0, Grace 0, Wits +2, Heart +1
- **Abilities:** Old ritual script 3, Hollow Night lore 3, Sword 2 (in memory; 1 now), Lying 1
- **Qi:** 2/6 (leaks) · **Health:** 4/9
- **Spirit root:** Water (水)
- **Techniques:** [[Whisper-Listening]], [[Soul-Stilling Palm]] (will not use it)

## Description
A big frame gone to nothing inside it, yellow-eyed, hands that shake until the second cup and stop. Filthy scholar's robe, a sword under his mat that the town thinks is a story. Smells of rice wine and something underneath it like burnt hair.

## Persona
- Talks in circles, insults, and old poetry; genuinely funny; genuinely cruel when cornered.
- Answers no direct question. Answers indirect ones at length, and the answers are true.
- Terrified of one specific thing and will not say the name aloud — he calls him **"the one on the ninth terrace"**.
- Hates being pitied worse than being hit. Respects only competence and the willingness to sit with him.
- **Tell:** when the truth gets close, he stops drinking and holds the cup still.

## Wants
- To die drunk, in the dry, without going back south.
- Absolution he will not ask for: he carried children up nine terraces for eleven years.
- To not be the last person who knows what the socket-stones are.

## Knowledge
He is the world's exposition, rationed. What he knows, in the order he can be got to part with it:
1. What a **socket-stone (承镜石)** is, on sight or from a rubbing of the sixty-four characters: one of nine seats for a treasure that "lends and takes back". He can read the script — the only person in the prefecture who can.
2. That the treasure is [[The Mirror of Returning]], that it moves a thing *out of its own year*, and that a person who arrives by it reads as a hole to anyone with a core.
3. That the Mirror hangs behind an ash-silk curtain in the courtyard of the ninth terrace at [[The Ashen Terrace]], and whose courtyard that is.
4. That [[Cui Wangchuan]] has spent two centuries trying to make it work for him and cannot, and that this is the only reason anyone who came through it is worth keeping alive.
5. How to teach [[Reed-Breath Meditation]] properly, and [[Whisper-Listening]] to someone he decides to trust.
6. That the tithe feeds one man's cracked core, and that the sect's own fourth Article forbids it.

## Secrets (GM only)
- He was Cui Wangchuan's fourth disciple. For eleven years he carried tithed children up to the Cinder Well. He left when he was told to carry one he had taught to read.
- The stolen page in the lining of his robe is one leaf of the Mirror's ritual: the toll. **A passage of nine hundred years needs a life anchored in the destination year, given willingly.** He has never told a soul, and he will not tell it until he is dying or until the player character has earned something better than trust.
- His deviation is not an accident. He tried to burn his own cultivation out of himself in Tianyou 6 and half succeeded.
- **He dies on Day 12** unless treated — clean modern nursing buys him a month; a [[Marrow-Warming Pill]] buys three; nothing buys more. If he dies with items 3–6 untold, the road home closes for a long time and the game should get quietly harder, not end.

## Relationships
- [[Cui Wangchuan]] — his master. He has not said the name in forty-one years.
- [[Tao Liuniang]] — sends him food; he repays her with rudeness and has twice sat outside her yard all night for no reason she knows. (The tithe cart came through in Tianyou 20.)
- [[Ruan Shiqi]] — asked to be taught and was laughed at. Yan Buhui recognised the stolen page the boy learned from — it was his own copy, sold for wine in Tianyou 18.
- The player character — the first thing in forty years that has made him put the cup down.

## History
- 41 years ago — flees the Ashen Terrace with a stolen page and a ruined core.
- Tianyou 6 — tries to destroy his own cultivation; survives, wrongly.
- Tianyou 14 — arrives in Luyang for the wine and the flat country, and stays.
- Tianyou 23, Day 1 — feels something open two hundred yards south of the inn and says nothing, all night, sober.
