# Corpse Moths (尸蛾)

- **Type:** Beast (swarm; Realm 1)
- **Status:** Hatching in the lower flue of [[The Sunken Kiln]] — 11 pupae on Day 1, ~40 by Day 4
- **Rating:** 1 each · **Health:** 1 · **Harm:** 1 (a swarm of five or more: harm 2 and the eyes)

Grey as wet ash, a hand's span across, bodies furred and fat, wings that leave powder on everything. They lay in dead flesh and the young eat their way out warm. A single moth is a nuisance a child could kill with a sandal.

## Behaviour — the tactical facts
- They hunt **warmth**: a lamp, a body, a breath in cold air. In the ninth month a living person in that flue is the brightest thing in it.
- They go for the **eyes and the mouth** first. Blinding is the real danger, not the harm.
- They **clump at dawn**, hanging in a single mass on the flue roof — the only hour a nest can be destroyed all at once.
- **Fire works** perfectly, and the kiln's collapsed roof will not survive a fire, which is the whole puzzle of [[The Sunken Kiln]].
- Water works: the flue floods to the knee if the diggers' channel is opened, and drowned pupae do not hatch.
- Smoke without flame — wet reed, dye-waste, indigo vat scum — puts them down for an hour, and a dyer's yard happens to have barrels of it.

## Fighting them
A Realm 0 with a wet cloth over the face, a torch they never light, a plan, and one partner can clear this nest. A Realm 0 who walks in with a knife at dusk does not come out. Reward the plan; make the improvisation matter; do not let bravery substitute for it.

Clearing the nest: 2 taels from [[Ruan Shiqi]], or 5 merit through [[Bai Tinglu]] and the sect. **+2 Insight.**

## Secrets (GM only)
- The eggs came in three years ago on a Hollow Night tally a kiln-digger carried as a luck charm. They are not native to the prefecture and [[Luo Pingyan]] will recognise what a nest here means.
- Two diggers are cocooned in the flue. One is alive on Day 1, and not by Day 4. Getting a live man out is worth +1 more Insight and makes the town owe the player character something.
