# Idris Vale

- **Type:** Character (player character — human, 40; they/them)
- **Status:** Alive
- **Location:** On the hill road, about to walk down into [[Dunmere]]
- **Concept:** A wandering water-diviner who heard that a whole valley had gone dry, and came to see why
- **Stats:** Heart +2, Grace +1, Wits 0, Might −1
- **Knack:** Dowsing and reading the land (+1 when it applies: finding water, reading weather, tracking, knowing plants and soil)
- **Health:** 6/6
- **Conditions:** none
- **Silver:** 10 silver, 0 copper

## Description
Lean and quiet, with sun-browned skin, grey-streaked black hair tied back with a leather cord, and a habit of crouching to touch the ground wherever they stop. They speak slowly and listen well.

## Inventory
- A forked hazel divining rod, polished by years of use
- A pouch of dried herbs and a small copper cup
- A waterskin, about half full
- A walking cloak and a bedroll

## Knows
- At Kettle Cross they heard that Merrowdale hasn't had a drop of rain since Midsummer, though the weather everywhere around it has been ordinary.
- The reeve of Dunmere has posted a notice offering **20 silver** for the return of the village's missing Rain Bell.
- Country folk in the west still honour spirits of wells, rivers, and lakes, whatever the Church says.
