# Agna Holt

- **Type:** Character (human woman, 48)
- **Status:** Alive
- **Location:** [[The Thirsty Heron]] — she owns it and lives upstairs
- **Role:** Innkeeper; the best-informed person in [[Dunmere]]
- **Abilities:** Gossip 3, Brewing 2, Brawling 1 (with a heavy ladle)

## Description
Broad-shouldered and red-cheeked, forearms like a smith's, grey hair in a no-nonsense braid. A loud laugh and a creative line in curses.

## Persona
- Warm but shrewd; sizes up newcomers in a glance. "Coin first, stories second."
- Loves gossip. Trades rumor for rumor, or for a round bought for the house.
- Can't stand Brother Caddock's sermons — bad for business, worse for the soul — but won't turn away a paying guest.
- A widow (her husband Jem died under an overturned cart six years ago). Soft on [[Wren]].

## Knowledge
- Everybody's business. When the PC fishes for gossip, roll on the Rumors table in [[The Thirsty Heron]].
- [[Fennick]] the peddler is back and camped in her yard.
- She suspects [[Corwin Hale]] has been taking extra water, but won't say so to a stranger.
- She's down to her last three casks of ale; after that, there's no water to brew more.

## Relationships
- [[Corwin Hale]] — old friend.
- [[Brother Caddock]] — her lodger, "a sermon on legs".
- [[Wren]] — feeds her scraps from the kitchen.
- [[Fennick]] — sells her water; she thinks he's a leech but pays anyway.

## History
- 1125 — takes over the Thirsty Heron from her father.
- 3 days before Day 1 — Caddock takes her best room and pays a month in advance.
