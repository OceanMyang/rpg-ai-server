# Ash Hounds (烬犬)

- **Type:** Beast (Realm 2, bonded to a handler)
- **Status:** Two pairs in the prefecture with [[Luo Pingyan]]'s hound-handlers
- **Rating:** 2 · **Health:** 5 · **Harm:** 2 · Always in pairs, never off the leash without a word

Hairless grey dogs the size of a calf, skin cracked like dried mud with a dull red glow down in the cracks. They do not bark. They breathe out heat, and they steam in the rain.

## What they are for
Tracking souls, not scents. An Ash Hound follows a person through water, through a crowd, over three days, by the shape of their qi. They are how the [[The Hollow Night Sect]] finds a hidden child, and why hiding [[Tao Xiaomai]] in a neighbour's loft does not work.

## Facts a player can use
- They track **qi**, so they cannot track a mortal at all — only cultivators and lit children. They lose a strong trail in a crowd of fifty mortals.
- They will not cross running water more than knee-deep; the handler must carry them, one at a time, and hates it.
- They are terrified of their own kind's death-cry. Killing one in front of the other ends the hunt for the day and costs the handler status.
- Killing a hound is a property crime the Hollow Night will invoice, precisely, to a household — and [[Luo Pingyan]] collects debts rather than revenge.
- **On a player character they are useless.** There is no qi-shape to follow. A hound put on their trail sits down and whines, and the handler reports it, and that report is what eventually reaches the ninth terrace.

## Secrets (GM only)
- The scar on [[Ruan Shiqi]]'s neck is from a hound in Tianyou 19. He has never told anyone why he was on the south road.
- Hounds are made, not bred: a child with a weak root and a dog, in the Cinder Well, over nine days. Anyone who learns this and tells a village changes what the tithe cart means forever.
