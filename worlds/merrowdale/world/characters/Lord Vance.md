# Lord Vance

- **Type:** Character (human man, 31)
- **Status:** Alive
- **Full name:** Edric Vance, Lord of Merrowdale
- **Location:** Vance Hall, a grey stone manor on the southern ridge above the Vance Weir, 1½ hours' walk from [[Dunmere]]
- **Role:** Liege lord of [[Merrowdale]], sworn to the crown of [[Aldermark]]
- **Abilities:** Swordplay 1, Law and letters 2; four armed household guards (rating 1 each)

## Description
Handsome in a soft, tired way, dressed in good clothes that have been mended more than once. Ink on his cuffs, wine on his breath by afternoon.

## Persona
- Proud and brittle; hates being seen as poor. Polite to people of rank, dismissive of peasants — until they have something he needs.
- Not cruel, but weak: he takes the easy road and tells himself it was the only one.
- Thinks the Lady of the Mere is a peasant superstition — and is privately a little afraid of the lake.

## Wants
- Money. He owes a fortune to a moneylender in Aldmere and could lose the Hall.
- The harvest tax from Dunmere, in full — which the drought makes impossible.

## Knowledge
- Vance Hall has a deep well that still gives clean water. He sells it by the barrel at 1 silver.
- Family papers in the Hall's library record that his great-great-grandfather built the weir "against the counsel of the Wendle priestess, who foretold the Lady's displeasure".
- He gave [[Brother Caddock]] a writ to hunt witches, mostly to be seen doing something.

## Secrets (GM only)
- If the drought is not over by the harvest (about 30 days after Day 1), he means to evict the Dunmere families who can't pay and sell their land to the moneylender.
- He could open the weir's sluice to send lake water down to the dry mill and the fields below it — but that would drain the lake faster, and he doesn't want the blame.

## Relationships
- [[Corwin Hale]] — his reeve in Dunmere; Vance thinks him soft.
- [[Brother Caddock]] — useful to him, for now.

## History
- 1141 — inherits Merrowdale and his father's debts.
- 4 days before Day 1 — grants Caddock a writ against witchcraft in Merrowdale.
