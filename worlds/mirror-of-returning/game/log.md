# Chronicle

Append-only, oldest first. One line each: `Day N, time — what happened`.

## Before play
- ~900 years ago — the [[The Mirror of Returning]] is cast, by whom nobody now agrees. Nine socket-stones are set across the prefectures to anchor it; [[Reedmouth Marsh]] holds the eighth.
- 212 years ago — [[Cui Wangchuan]] takes the Mirror out of a drowned tomb, and stops aging.
- 96 years ago — the [[The Hollow Night Sect]] takes the Ashen Terrace by force and begins the soul-tithe in the southern prefectures.
- 41 years ago — [[Yan Buhui]] flees the Ashen Terrace with a stolen page and a ruined core, and drinks his way north.
- 31 years ago — [[Gu Shuye]] becomes head of the [[The Green Bamboo Sect]] with eleven disciples and a mountain nobody wants.
- 3 years ago, spring — [[Tao Liuniang]]'s husband drowns dredging the dye channel; she keeps the yard alone.
- 3 years ago, ninth month — the tithe reaches [[Cangping Prefecture]] for the first time. Four children leave [[Luyang Town]] on the cart; none come back.
- 21 days before Day 1 — kiln-diggers break into [[The Sunken Kiln]] and something lays eggs in the flue.
- 2 days before Day 1 — [[Luo Pingyan]] arrives in the prefecture seat to prepare this year's tithe.
